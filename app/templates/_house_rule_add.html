A correction to an item

Open the item from a character's page and use Correct this at the bottom of its side panel, with This campaign picked. It shows here, and only this campaign changes.

One roll for all darts

The rules roll each dart of Magic Missile on its own. Many tables roll once and every dart deals that damage.

A ruling on a spell

A short note the roll card shows whenever someone rolls this spell, e.g. "Fireball: the DM decides who is in the area on the Zoom map."

The list has every spell askdnd has an audited roll recipe for. A spell already ruled on gets its ruling replaced.

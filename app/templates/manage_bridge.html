What each connection can change

Own sheet green

The player's sheet is open in their Chrome with the bridge (a background tab counts). It always goes first.

For example: a laptop with the sheet in one tab and askdnd in another.

Through the DM yellow

The player's sheet isn't open, and the DM has any D&D Beyond page open in Chrome with the bridge. Only for characters in the campaign's D&D Beyond game.

For example: the player is on an iPhone or forgot their sheet, and the DM has the VTT open. The DM's copy of the player's sheet works the same; the changes show there as they land.

Neither red, or grey

Neither is open: askdnd refuses a change and says how to reach the sheet. One already on its way waits (the wait timer below), then is listed to make by hand.

For example: the player's sheet is closed and the DM is on an iPad; or a test copy.

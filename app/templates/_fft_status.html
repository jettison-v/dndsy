{# Pausing asks why, optional (Brett's pick, Nov 19): the bar then says it, so anyone looking knows #}
Claude listening
While a Claude session runs, what you send reaches Claude within minutes. When none is, it waits for the next one: open the Claude app and say "check askdnd".
A round is open
Nothing merges to askdnd.ai until you end it; Claude only sorts what you send meanwhile.
Deploys: After my local try
You're around: visible changes wait for your try on your Mac before they deploy (a Try on your Mac item in For you, and a notification). It goes back to When tests pass after 2 hours, or sooner if you're quiet in askdnd for 30 minutes or a try waits 30 minutes.
Deploys: When tests pass
The default. Claude deploys visible changes once their tests pass, and you try them on the live site afterwards. Careful changes (who sees what, sign-in, spending, the D&D Beyond bridge) still wait for your try first.
Deploys: Paused
Nothing deploys to the live site until you choose another, for whatever reason you give. Claude goes on building on branches.

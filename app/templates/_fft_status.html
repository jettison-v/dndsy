Claude listening
While a Claude session runs, what you send reaches Claude within minutes. When none is, it waits for the next one: open the Claude app and say "check askdnd".
A round is open
Nothing merges to askdnd.ai until you end it; Claude only sorts what you send meanwhile.
Local Testing
You're around: visible changes wait for your try on your Mac before they merge (a Try on your Mac item in For you, and a notification). It ends after 2 hours, or sooner if you're quiet in askdnd for 30 minutes or a try waits 30 minutes.
Independent Work
The default. Claude merges visible changes when their tests pass, and you try them on askdnd.ai after they're live. Careful changes (who sees what, sign-in, spending, the D&D Beyond bridge) still wait for your try first.
Deploy Freeze
Game night: nothing deploys to askdnd.ai until you choose another. Claude goes on building on branches.

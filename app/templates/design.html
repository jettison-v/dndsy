Space and corners

Padding, margins and gaps come from a 4px scale. Controls are --control-h tall: 32px on desktop, 44px on phones. Layouts change at 640px (phone), 900px (tablet) and 1100px (wide).

Inline spacing isn't used on any page. Where a block's parent isn't a .row or .stack (whose gap spaces it), a helper does: .mt-1….mt-5 space above, .mb-1….mb-3 below, .m-0 none, .byline a note tucked under what it's about ("Added by Big C · Oct 26"), .push a button at the far end of its row, .grow the part of a row that takes the room left, .row.tight small controls that belong together.

Narrow screens

From 375px phones to wide monitors, nothing scrolls sideways, one-line controls stay on one line, and text stays in its box. Each part decides what gives way first. A browser test checks every page at 375, 768, 1000 and 1280px on every change (tests/browser/test_layout.py); docs/DESIGN.md › Narrow screens has the rules.

PartAs the room runs out
Menu barBelow 1240px Live shortens to "● Live"; then the campaign's name ends in "…"; below 1100px the word askdnd goes; at 900px and below, the bottom bar takes over. Manage, your character, Party, ? and you never wrap.
MenusTheir own width, never wider than the window; text wraps inside. Party's opens leftward.
Side panelBeside the page on wide screens; a sheet from the bottom at 900px and below.
Lists with columnsCompact rows whenever the list's own box is narrower than its columns need (Spells 920px, Wild Shape 740, Features 700, Inventory 680), whatever the screen.
Tab bars in sectionsReach the section's edges: 30px out on wide screens, 18px from tablet down.
ButtonsOne line always (.btn is nowrap); the row around them wraps.
Long wordsBreak rather than widen the page (a web address in a note).

Narrow screens

From 375px phones to wide monitors, nothing scrolls sideways, one-line controls stay on one line, and text stays in its box. Each part decides what gives way first. A browser test checks every page at 375, 768, 1000 and 1280px on every change (tests/browser/test_layout.py); docs/DESIGN.md › Narrow screens has the rules.

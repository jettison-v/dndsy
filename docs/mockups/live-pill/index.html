The Live pill: what "connected" looks like

The pill answers one question: will a change you make in askdnd reach your D&D Beyond sheet? Every state says what to do in words, except the good one, which is a green dot alone, and that didn't say what it meant. The DM's count ("4 of 14") didn't say 4 of what either. Here is every state, today and three ways to say it, at its real size in the menu bar.

Round 1, side by side

What each is better at

A · Your sheet, with a badge (round 1: you read it as a document)
  • Says what's connected: your sheet, the thing the question is about.
  • The same mark in every state, only the badge changes (✓ green, … amber, ! red), so the pill reads at a glance and the words carry the rest.
  • The DM's count is a stack of sheets: "4 of 14" reads as sheets.
  • One more icon to learn, though it's the sheet you already have open.
B · The dot, with a word
  • "Connected" in plain words; nothing to learn.
  • Wider: the word costs about 90px, on a laptop and a phone.
  • Doesn't say connected to what.
C · A plug
  • Reads as "connected" across apps.
  • Suggests a cable or power more than your D&D Beyond sheet; unplugged for the bad states looks like an error even when the DM carries it.

Cast it to see what changes

A corner card says it's done, with Undo, as for every change; Battle, Party, the fight and the World follow.

The rules it follows

  • One familiar at a time: casting while it's here gives it a new form
  • After 0 Hit Points it reappears only when the spell is cast again
  • Celestial, Fey or Fiend instead of a Beast, chosen when cast
  • A Ritual takes 10 minutes longer and spends no slot
  • The incense is said, never checked: the DM rules

A Druid's Wild Companion opens the same window from Wild Shape's uses (Fey only, gone at the next Long Rest); a Warlock's Pact of the Chain adds its forms and needs no slot.

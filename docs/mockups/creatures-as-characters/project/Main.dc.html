A familiar, a steed, a Homunculus: a small character of its own

Brett, Nov 4: "a simplified character that the summoner or creator controls", in battle and in the story, never an afterthought. Where you'll find it: everywhere a character shows today, the creature shows too, as its master's: Battle (the switch by the name, built), Party, the fight's rows and band, Table rolls, and Campaign › World. It comes and goes as its rules say: casting brings it, and dismissing it, 0 Hit Points, a Long Rest or the end of Concentration send it away. The example is Trah's familiar, Pip.

What makes it a character

  • Its own name and picture, kept for the whole campaign, whatever form it takes
  • Its own Hit Points, kept on D&D Beyond (built)
  • Numbers worked out: from its form, the spell's level or its master's level, with the source
  • Its turn by its rules: its own Initiative, or right after its master
  • Here only while the rules say: cast, dismissed, 0 Hit Points, a Long Rest, Concentration
  • Its story: an entry in the World, linked to its master

Its master plays it: their player rolls for it and changes it; the DM can, as for any character.

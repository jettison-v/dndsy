D&D Beyond keeps the creature; askdnd keeps the companion

Checked Nov 4 on our table's sheets and in D&D Beyond's own page: each creature on the Extras tab is one stat block in a group (Familiar, Infusion, Summoned…) with a name, notes, its Hit Points and an Active switch on the Extras list. D&D Beyond's page can add, rename, remove and switch one, and change its size, creature type, alignment, AC, Hit Points and notes. It has no picture of its own (always the stat block's) and no idea of Pip as one familiar across forms. All 16 creatures at our table are switched off today.

On D&D Beyond (Extras)

  • The creature: one entry per form, in its group (Pip as an Owl is an Owl named Pip under Familiar)
  • Its Hit Points, taken and temporary · synced, built
  • Its maximum, where it grows with its master: set by askdnd when it first reads the creature and whenever its master's level or Intelligence changes, with a corner card and Undo; never over a number someone typed on D&D Beyond · today a button, the yellow line
  • Its creature type: Celestial, Fey or Fiend, D&D Beyond's own override · new
  • Active: switched on while it's here, off when it's away or gone · new
  • Its name and notes, as the player types them there

So a player who opens D&D Beyond sees the same: which familiar is here, its Hit Points and type. Its own rolls still work there.

In askdnd

  • The companion: Pip, one being across its forms (the Raven entry and the Owl entry are both Pip)
  • Its picture, added by its player, kept for the campaign
  • Where it is: here, the pocket dimension, gone (D&D Beyond's Active only says here or not)
  • What ends it: a Long Rest, Concentration, 0 Hit Points; and its turn reminders
  • Numbers worked out from the spell's level or its master's level, with the source
  • Its story: the World entry, from session recaps you review

Like Wild Shape: D&D Beyond keeps the list and the numbers it can; which one is in play, and why, is askdnd's.

How askdnd uses D&D Beyond when Pip is cast · Brett chose A (Nov 4)

A · askdnd keeps Extras in step

Chosen
  • Casting adds the form to Extras if it isn't there, named Pip, with its type; switches it Active and any other familiar off
  • Away or gone switches it off; back switches it on
  • Never deletes: an old form stays on the list, switched off, so D&D Beyond's list is also Pip's forms
  • Needs: the add, rename, type and Active requests tried on a test sheet, and extension 0.11.0 (another Google review)

B · Extras is the player's list; askdnd reads it

  • The player adds each form on D&D Beyond themselves (as you did for Levi); Find Familiar offers only forms already there, plus "Add it on D&D Beyond first"
  • Here, away and gone live in askdnd only; D&D Beyond's Active stays as it is
  • askdnd writes only Hit Points (built)
  • Nothing new for the extension, but D&D Beyond's page won't show who's here

Either way: the picture and the story are askdnd's, and a creature on Extras that was never cast through askdnd (Levi's Owl today) shows on its master's Battle as Not here · Cast Find Familiar, not as here.

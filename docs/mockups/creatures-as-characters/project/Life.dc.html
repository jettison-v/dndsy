Dismiss Pip forever?

Pip leaves Trah's service. Its World entry stays, as Trah's familiar that was; casting Find Familiar again brings a new one. On D&D Beyond the Owl stays on the Extras list, switched off.

On Trah's own Battle

Pip's card in his Bonus Action column, See through Pip's eyes, and Deliver a touch spell under Reaction while Pip is here. Gone or away: they're greyed with the reason, as Sheet does for a form.

Undo, as everywhere

Each of these makes a corner card with Undo: sent away, back, dismissed, and 0 Hit Points (in case the damage was a mistake).

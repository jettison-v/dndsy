Choices still made on D&D Beyond

Changing your options in askdnd

Besides leveling up, a few choices are still changed only on D&D Beyond, three of them after a Long Rest. Each gets a window like Prepare spells: it says the rule, never enforces it, and makes the change on the sheet through the bridge, with Undo. Plus one question: maximum Hit Points (Aid).

Where they liveEach where you'd look for it: Wild Shape's forms on Battle and Sheet › Wild Shape; weapon masteries on Sheet › Actions; cantrips and spells on Sheet › Spells and the Long Rest window.
Who uses themThe character's player, and the DM for anyone.
Before buildingEach needs D&D Beyond's own request recorded on a test seat, as Prepare spells did. Weapon masteries likely need a new extension permission (another Chrome Web Store review).

1 · Wild Shape's known forms

Swap a form

The forms you know, and the ones you could learn (from the Wild Shape list askdnd already shows). Tick one out, one in; one Make for the swap. Offered after a Long Rest (the Long Rest window links to it), and any time from Sheet › Wild Shape for the DM's say-so.

3 and 4 · Cantrips, copied spells, new spells

A Wizard's spellbook, and spells learned at a new level

One window with three tabs: swap a cantrip (after a Long Rest), copy a spell into the spellbook (a scroll or another book, with its cost), and the spells a new level brings (a Wizard's two, a Warlock's or Sorcerer's new ones), offered by the level-up card on Battle.

5 · A question for you

Maximum Hit Points from askdnd (Aid)

Almost everything that changes in play already goes both ways with D&D Beyond. The gap is maximum Hit Points going up or down (Aid, a curse, a bonus): askdnd reads it but can't set it, so casting Aid in askdnd leaves everyone's maximum to be changed on D&D Beyond. Adding it is small once D&D Beyond's request is recorded.

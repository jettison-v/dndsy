Title

Where it is

Its round trip

    Every hop comes from askdnd's own record: who did what, when. Nothing here is typed by hand.

    How the queue feeds the board

    The queue is everything not started: Waiting, then Ready. The board is its front and everything in flight. Every move is askdnd applying a rule to a fact (your OK arrived, a claim went quiet, tests passed), so nothing moves because someone remembered to move it.

    How to read it

    Ready is in my execution order, each card saying why in one line; your Roadmap order is the input, and if you want something first, you say so in chat or on the item. Building holds one piece, with my last check-in (any call I make to askdnd counts, so a long test run doesn't look quiet); quiet for 20 minutes, it turns amber and goes back to Ready. The Monday phone sweep's session claims its own Upkeep card the same way. Shipping is everything with a pull request, its step under the title (In review, Merging, Live and being tried by me). Yours to try holds both kinds of try: your local try before a merge (Careful, or Look while Deploys is After my local try) and a check once it's live. A merge held by Pause says so on its card. A red pull request, one behind main, a try waiting too long, or a Now item behind less important work for a week turns amber where it sits. Click a card for its round trip.

    Settled by the scope

    This page first asked six picks. The scope (v2, then v4) answers them, so they're no longer yours to answer here:

    1. Where: Development's first tab, Board.
    2. A card: its kind, title, one line of why, and amber when stuck; the round trip has the rest.
    3. The round trip: in every item's window, from askdnd's own record.
    4. Ready's order: mine, with its why. No Pin: you say “this first” in chat or on the item. A Now item behind less important work for a week turns amber.
    5. The kinds: icon, word and coloured edge. No filter, until the board is long enough to need one.
    6. The Queue tab: folded into the Board.

Title

Where it is

Its round trip

    Every hop comes from askdnd's own record: who did what, when. Nothing here is typed by hand.

    How the queue feeds the board

    The queue is everything not started: Waiting, then Ready. The board is its front and everything in flight. Every move is askdnd applying a rule to a fact (your OK arrived, a claim went quiet, tests passed), so nothing moves because someone remembered to move it.

    How to read it

    Ready is in my execution order, each card saying why it's there, with Pin to put it first (your pin wins). A card in Being built shows who holds it and their last check-in; one quiet for 20 minutes turns amber and goes back to Ready. A red pull request, a merge stuck behind main, or a try waiting too long turns amber where it sits. Click a card for its round trip.

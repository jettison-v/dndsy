One switch for every yes/no setting

You chose the switch, always at the row's right, saving itself the moment it's flipped. Today a yes/no setting on Manage is one of five things: a switch on the right (Books, Features, Email digest), a switch on the left (Labs, Test campaign), a checkbox and Save (Accounts' Admin, Art style's Describe it), a button saying its state (Accounts' "Sends feedback: on"), and radios and Save (Trophy Case). The piece

A setting row, and the rules it keeps

  • A setting that takes effect now is a switch at its row's right: green and to the right when on. Its name says what's on, never "on/off" in words beside it.
  • Flipped, it moves at once and a corner card names it ("Labs: on"); the page doesn't reload or jump. If it can't save, an amber card says why and the switch goes back.
  • Off-limits (not yours to change), it's dimmed and its line says who changes it.
  • A choice of two or three that also takes effect now (Trophy Case) is the two-way switch, saving on press, at the same right edge.
  • Not a switch: a choice inside a form you're filling in, saved by its own button (Add an account's Admin, Import's "Keep the current descriptions", Table's test copies) stays a checkbox; Dice tuning's toggles stay with its sliders and their Save, because you try them before saving.

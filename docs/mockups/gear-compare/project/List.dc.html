How a row is worked out

Weapons against the one in hand (Quarterstaff here), armor against what gives his AC now (Unarmored Defense), a magic item that's worn against nothing: what it adds. The same sums as board 1, cut to what changes most.

What it never does

Sort or mark a "best" item, or hide one that's worse. A ▼ is a fact, like the triangle for a catch today; the player and the DM decide.

On a phone

The chips go under the item's name on its row; the details open over the list as they do now.

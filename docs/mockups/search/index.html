Search, like Spotlight

One search for anything about your character, from any page: Search in the menu bar (always there, so it's found without knowing the key), or ⌘ F (Ctrl F on Windows; it takes over the browser's find, which stays one step away) or ⌘ K. A box opens in the middle; results come as you type, grouped by kind, the best already highlighted. ↑ ↓ move, Enter acts, Esc closes.

Where each kind of result goes

Your characterActions, spells, features, items, resources and conditions. Enter does it: rolls an attack or a check (the roll card), opens Cast for a spell, Use for an item. ⌘ Enter opens its row on Battle or its side panel instead.
Checks, saves, skillsWhat Table rolls' Roll finds today, now from anywhere: "ste" rolls Stealth.
RulesConditions and rule terms (the SRD's and the books your campaign has): Enter opens the rule in the side panel, as an underlined term does.
The storyPeople, places and things in the World, key moments, awards and recaps, your notes. Enter opens it where it lives (the World entry, the recap at that moment).
Pages"party", "inbox", "dice": Enter goes there, as Spotlight opens an app.

Two choices for you

1. What Enter does · my pick: AA, the thing itself (roll, Cast, Use, open the page), with ⌘ Enter to open where it lives. B, always open where it lives first, and you press the roll there: one more step, never a surprise roll.
2. Whose things · my pick: AA, your own character, the rules and the story everyone shares. B, the party's characters too (their spells and items, read only, as Party shows them).

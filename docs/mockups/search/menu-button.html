Search in the menu bar: three ways

Search lives at the right of the menu bar, between ⚙ and ?, on every page. Today it's drawn like a text box (Search ⌘F, 128 pixels wide), but clicking it opens a separate box in the middle of the screen. You like that it says ⌘F opens it; it's large, and it looks like something you'd type into. Below, today's and three others, each at a 13–14" laptop's width with the buttons beside it. Click the search in a bar to see what it opens.

Now: a field that isn't one

Story

A · A button that shows the key You chose this · built

A magnifier and ⌘F, drawn as a button like Manage and ⚙, not as a field. Half the width (about 70 pixels), it still tells you the key, and nothing about it says "type here", so the box opening in the middle is what a button does. The box stays where it is: in the middle, as Spotlight does it.

Story
GoodKeeps the ⌘F hint you like, at half the width; looks like what it is, a button.
CostsThe word "Search" goes (the magnifier and the hover tip say it).

B · Just the magnifier

A round button like ? beside it, 36 pixels. The key shows on hover ("Search · ⌘F") and inside the box. The quietest bar; the key is easy to forget, which matters because ⌘F is the quickest way in.

Story
Search · ⌘F
GoodSmallest; matches ? and the account picture.
CostsThe ⌘F hint is only on hover. A new player may not know the key.

C · Keep the field, and make it one

It stays a field, and clicking it lets you type right there: it grows wider in place and the results open under it, so nothing jumps to the middle. ⌘F does the same, from any page.

Story
GoodWhat you see is what it does: you type where you clicked.
CostsStill 128 pixels in the bar. Results sit at the right edge, far from the middle of the page, where the roll window then opens. More work: a second way to draw the box.

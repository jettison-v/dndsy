Hide and Ready, kept on Battle

Trent's note: "Can Hide and Ready have Effects? Ready would ideally show what action you are Readying." You chose to show the result on Battle: once you've hidden, Battle says so until it ends, and once you've readied something, Battle shows what and when. Drawn here with Trah. askdnd informs and keeps track; the DM still rules on whether you can hide and when a trigger happens.

Where it lives

Battle's Action column, "What anyone can do"

Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, Influence, Magic, Ready, Search, Study, UtilizeToday each opens its rule in the side panel, with the checks it rolls (Hide's DC 15 Stealth). Nothing stays on Battle afterwards.

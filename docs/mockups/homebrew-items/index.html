Homebrew items

Your note (Nov 10), after setting up Trah's Beasty Bone: the DM should make and manage the campaign's own items in askdnd, not through a setting tucked into one item's side panel. What an item does in play is picked from what askdnd already plays (a roll, an effect, charges, a creature it brings), so its rules are set once when it's made, not worked out at the table.

Where it livesTo decide (below): a new DM Tools page, or Manage › the campaign's pages. Either way, one list of the campaign's own items.
Who uses itThe campaign's DM (and admins). Players see an item once it's on their sheet, as any item.
Three decisionsWhere it lives, whether an item also goes onto D&D Beyond's sheet, and whether players can find these items. Each has my pick below.

The page and the item

One list, and an item made from askdnd's own pieces

A list of the campaign's items (who has each, ready, retired). Each item has its book fields and its words, then What it does in play: pieces askdnd already plays, added with +: a roll (it plays on the roll card), charges and when they come back, an effect, a bonus to another roll, a creature it brings (as the Beasty Bone does now), a spell it casts. Give to a character puts it on their sheet.

Build: M (the pieces exist; the page, the item form and giving are new)
1 · Where it lives
AManage › the campaign, beside House rules and Settings. My pick: the DM already goes there for the campaign's rules, and it needs no new door in the menu bar.
BA new DM Tools page, the start of step 19's DM Tools. Worth it once there are several tools; today it would be one page behind a new door.
2 · Onto the D&D Beyond sheet too?
AYes, as a custom item, with its name and words, so the sheet stays complete for anyone who opens it on D&D Beyond. askdnd keeps what it does in play. My pick, while the table still uses D&D Beyond's sheets.
BNo, askdnd only. Simpler, but D&D Beyond's sheet misses the item.
3 · Can players find them?
AOnly once the DM gives one. My pick: homebrew items are usually rewards, and finding them early would spoil it.
BIn the item browser, marked "This campaign's", for items the DM sells in a shop.
CA per-item switch, "Players can find it", off unless the DM turns it on.

The idea · a backup for a closed sheet

The DM carries a change when a sheet is closed

Where you'll find it: nothing new to press. It shows in the Live pill in the menu bar, for the player and for the DM, and the DM switches it on once in Manage › Settings › Virtual tabletop. The test on the real D&D Beyond showed the DM's account can already make these changes.

askdnd holds itOnly the player's browser may collect it

Today, sheet closed
A change in askdndFrom anyone allowed to make it
Refused"Open your sheet to connect"
Nothing reaches D&D Beyond. It's made there by hand, or not at all.
With the backup
A change in askdndThe same people, the same rules
askdnd holds itNo open sheet: the DM's browser may collect it
Any D&D Beyond page the DM has openMakes it, with the DM's rights
D&D Beyondaskdnd reads it back, as always

What it needs

  • The DM's Chrome has the bridge and is signed in to D&D Beyond.
  • Any D&D Beyond page open there. The VTT counts.
  • The character is in that D&D Beyond campaign.
  • The DM switched it on for the campaign.

What stays the same

  • Who may change what in askdnd. The DM carries; it gives no one new rights.
  • A player's own open sheet always goes first.
  • A change counts only once askdnd reads it back.
  • One batch at a time per character, so two browsers can't double a change.

What it can't do

  • A roll won't reach D&D Beyond's Game Log: that takes the player's own sheet.
  • D&D Beyond won't say who made it. askdnd's log will.
  • If the sheet and the DM's page are both closed, it's refused as today.
  • Test copies stay out, as they do now.

Still to prove

  • Tested: Hit Points, Inspiration, a slot, a resource, a condition, on D&D Beyond's own page.
  • Not yet: making them from another page with the sheet closed.
  • Not yet: death saves, rests, items, coins. Any that fail stay "open your sheet".
  • It needs a new extension version, through Google's review.

A bonus: the bridge can't run on a phone, so a player on a phone can't connect at all today. With the backup, their changes reach D&D Beyond too.

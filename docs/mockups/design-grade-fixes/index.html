Fix 4 · a direction, not one change

Lean on what's askdnd's own

The story's scroll, the thrown dice and the condition icons are what make askdnd feel like itself. Use them more where they fit: the scroll's paper behind the recap wherever it shows (Story, a session, Listen), and the dice's colours in Table rolls. A direction for later pages, not a rebuild.

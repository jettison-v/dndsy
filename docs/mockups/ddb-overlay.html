askdnd beside D&D Beyond

A small askdnd extension in Chrome adds askdnd's panel to your own signed-in D&D Beyond tab. It hears every change on the sheet at once, and when you act in askdnd it makes the change on D&D Beyond, as you. Shown with Cole, after a hit from Enemy 1 (37 of 51 HP), drinking one of his two Potions of Healing.

D&D Beyond confirms every change.askdnd never shows a number as changed until it has read the change back from D&D Beyond. Until then the change says it's waiting, and if it can't be made, askdnd says so and shows where to make it. The two never quietly disagree.

What changed

    Everywhere in askdnd

    Four states, never a fifth

    Every number that lives on D&D Beyond carries one of these, on the panel, on Battle, on Party and on the fight screen.

    Live with D&D Beyond

    What you see is what D&D Beyond has, read moments ago.

    Waiting for D&D Beyond

    You've made a change in askdnd; it's being made on D&D Beyond. Usually a second or two. The old number stays until it's confirmed.

    Not on D&D Beyond yet

    It couldn't be made: the extension is off, you're on a phone, or D&D Beyond changed. askdnd keeps the change listed until it appears there.

    Changed on D&D Beyond

    Someone changed it on D&D Beyond itself. askdnd shows it at once and adds it to What changed.

    Without the extension

    On a phone, askdnd lists what to make

    Chrome on a phone can't run extensions, so askdnd can't make the change itself. It keeps the change as Not on D&D Beyond yet, with a button that opens the right place there, and ticks it off by itself once D&D Beyond has it. Nothing else changes until then: the potion still counts as two everywhere.

    The DM

    Loot goes into D&D Beyond's Party Inventory

    The DM hands out loot from askdnd, and the DM's extension puts it in D&D Beyond's Party Inventory, the shared space next to each player's own. Players take it from there, on D&D Beyond or through askdnd, so loot never lives only in askdnd.

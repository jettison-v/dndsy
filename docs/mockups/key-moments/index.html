The key moment

One piece, and the rules it keeps

  • The slot: the kind (Decision blue, Reveal violet, Loot amber, Event and Theory grey) and the date as the table knows it, "Session 13 · Nov 6". On a recap the date is left off: every moment there is that session's.
  • The names it mentions go under it, linked (they open the side panel). On an entry, the entry's own name is left out (it's about them); a hidden entry's name is never there (the standing rule).
  • A Theory is in italics, in grey: the party's guess, not what happened.
  • How many: a recap shows its major moments (8 on Previously on…, 3 on Sessions, as now); an entry, a quest and the side panel show the newest 3. Then "Show the other N key moments", which opens the rest in the same list (it never moves what you were reading). Fewer than 5: all of them, no fold.
  • The order: a recap reads as the session went; everywhere else, newest first.
  • A quest that's open keeps one mark of its own: its newest moment's dot is amber, "where it stands". A done quest's dots are all the same.
  • None yet is the one empty state: "No key moments mention Cole yet." (a quest: "No progress yet.").
Every place, every state

Where key moments go

Widths are each place's own on a 1280px laptop with Table rolls open, and a 375px phone.

The fold

"+ Add what the party learned", and the rules it keeps

  • Closed until pressed, in link red, under What the table knows (an entry, a quest, the side panel), at the foot of a recap (in place of "Suggest a correction or addition"), and on a glossary entry. A quest's opens in place: it no longer takes you to its entry.
  • Open, the fold's line gives way to the form: two choice chips, Add something and Something here is wrong (the design system's chips, as in the feedback window; Add something first everywhere, where recaps had it the other way round; in a narrow column the second wraps under the first), the box, then Send and Cancel. Cancel, or Escape, closes it. What you typed stays until you send it.
  • Who sees it is said beside Send: a player's "The DM or a reviewer adds it."; the DM's button is Add, "You're the DM, so it goes straight on."
  • After Send the fold closes, a corner card says it's sent, and it shows under Your suggestions here as Waiting (as now). The DM's goes straight onto What the table knows.
  • On a phone the box is 16px (Safari doesn't zoom) and the buttons 44px tall.

    The two ways

    A. Soon, then quiet (my pick). The first new thing starts a 30-minute wait; then one email with all of it. No other email until the Inbox has been opened. A busy night is one email; a quiet week is none.

    B. Once a day. At a set hour (say 6 PM), one email if anything arrived since the last. Predictable, but a question can wait almost a day.

    Either way: never more than one email at a time waiting, and none for what you did yourself (the DM's own changes don't count).

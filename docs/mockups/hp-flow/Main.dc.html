Changing Hit Points without a roll

Where you'll find it: the Hit points tile at the top of Battle. This is for damage with no roll to apply, like a trap. Apply to, on a roll in Table rolls, already goes straight to step 3.

Step 1Click the Hit points tileA side panel opens that mostly explains the number.
Step 2Find Damage, Heal or Temporary HPThree buttons at the top of that panel. Nothing on the tile says they're there.
Step 3A second window opensIt asks Damage, Heal or Temporary HP again.
Step 4Type the number, pick a typeThe working appears here.
Step 5Take itThe corner card says what changed.

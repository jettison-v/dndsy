The number it gets

Room betweenA whole number between its new neighbours: between 15 and 12, 13. No whole numberHalf way: between 12 and 11, 11.5. D&D Beyond keeps it and orders by it (tried on the Mac Mini, Nov 4). Between a tieBetween two at 12, the one below drops by a half and it goes between (11.75). Shown on the line before you let go. No one elseMoves only what you dragged, and its creature that takes its turn right after it.

Where the change goes

Into the VTT's sidebar through the DM's open VTT (extension 0.12.0), and to every open askdnd page. The corner card says On its way, then On D&D Beyond, with Undo. The VTT closed: "Made once the VTT is open", the bridge's own words.

Also

Press a number to type one instead, as in the VTT. Whose turn it is stays theirs. Players see the same rows, without handles. On a phone, press and hold a row to drag, or use ↑ ↓.

SESSION 19 · NOV 2

The road to Star Mount

On the road to Star Mount, the party met a group led by NPC 1 and NPC 2. Each bore a mark on the forehead, showed no fear of the dark, and urged the party to turn back.

Trah answered with fire. When the smoke cleared only NPC 2 was left standing, dazed, and remembering nothing of the weeks before. Big C tended her burns while No Name kept watch on the hills.

Cole found a red stone in the ashes. It flickered when he held it, and the party carried on toward the monastery with more questions than answers.

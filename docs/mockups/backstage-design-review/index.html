Backstage, page by page: what we found

Every Backstage page outside Our work (24 of them; Our work's four had their own pass, which you've picked on), photographed on a laptop-wide screen and reviewed by three reviewers who had never seen askdnd, each against the page's own job and docs/DESIGN.md, then by me. Scene notes wasn't reviewed: it's the DM's alone, and askdnd's table is off on this campaign. Nothing changes until you pick.

How the pages scoredOut of 5 for doing their one job: four 4s (Needs you, Table, Features, Dice tuning), most 3s, and seven 2s: Documents, Backups, Places, Settings, both Histories, the Design system.
The big findingMost problems repeat across pages: lists built five different ways, status colours that disagree, alarms in small grey text, forms always open. Fixing each once, in the design system, fixes every page that has it.
Your earlier notes, here"No filters" on Documents (issue 5) is D1; "the radius on these larger pills is off" (issue 6) is S6: a status pill wraps into a blob, so it's fixed once, for every page.

Simply wrong: I'll fix these with the first build, no pick needed

  • Sessions' "Draft all 9 at once" spends money with one plain press: it asks first, saying the cost.
  • The bridge's "Check everything" changes the real test sheet with no confirm; its timers' reset too.
  • Documents decides a colour by a status's words, against the rule that nothing decides by display text.
  • The Design system page misstates its own sizes (Table mode's numbers, drawn in Backstage mode).
  • Dice tuning's preview says "Press Throw"; the buttons say "Throw a d20".
  • Features' legend promises deploy lines it doesn't draw.

4 · How we work through it

You send as you go; Claude picks them up when you say so

Nothing interrupts the work in progress: notes wait in the queue until you say "review the feedback" in this chat, at the end of a section of your review.

You, any timeSendOne sentence from the page, and point or paste if it helps. Small things too: they cost a few seconds now.
→
You, in this chat"Review the feedback"When a section of your review is done. Claude reads the queue from askdnd, with every screenshot and page.
→
ClaudeGroups and asksLike notes become one fix ("Battle on a phone"), marked Planned. What's unclear gets a question on the note itself: Question for you.
ClaudeSays the plan hereA short list in this chat: the groups, what each fixes, what's waiting on your answers. Bigger ones get a mockup first, as now.
→
Claude, then youFixes, you try itBuilt and tried locally or on beta, as today, with Try it yourself.
→
ClaudeMarks it DoneWith the PR that fixed it, so the queue shows what happened to every note. Delete only when you say.

What Claude gets for each note

private/feedback/42/ (never committed)
note.md your words, and any answers
shot.png the page as you opened the window
pasted.png your own screenshot, if you added one
page.html the page as drawn
context.json where, when, screen, browser, extension

The time finds the moment in beta's logs, and the extension's version answers "had it updated yet?" at once.

What it takes to set up

One step for you: a key for Claude, set once with scripts/set-secret.sh (it goes into Railway and your computer, never into the chat or git). Claude can't sign in to beta as you, so its script reads the queue with that key.

Kept like the rest of askdnd: admins only, in askdnd's own database and files on beta, never in git or Sentry (the notes hold campaign pages). The extension isn't needed: no new permissions, nothing for Google to review.

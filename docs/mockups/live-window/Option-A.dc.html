Good

The shortest window, and it reads like a table: every dot, name, word and ↗ sits in its own column.

The cost

The words have to stay short to fit one line. "updated 14 hours ago" becomes "· 14 h", and a longer status (a reason something couldn't refresh) goes in the ↗'s hover or gets cut off. On a narrow phone the name gets the squeeze.

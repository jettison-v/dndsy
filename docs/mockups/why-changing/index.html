Why you're changing

In Change spells and Change forms, Why are you changing? decides which of the 2024 rules apply now: it marks each list Now or Not now, and a swap past a rule shows amber. Today it's three small chips in the window's header, beside the title, in the same grey as a date, so it's easy to miss, and the window opens on Just looking, which marks nothing. Big C shows it best: his Paladin spells change after a Long Rest, his Sorcerer ones only on a Sorcerer level. Every board works: press a reason and watch his two cards.

Today

Where it is now

The reasons share the header with the title and ✕. Nothing tells you to pick one, and nothing changes until you do.

A · The reason first, in the window's bodyMy pick

A reason bar above the class cards

The question moves out of the header to the first thing in the window, at normal size, as the design system's switch (the one Battle · Sheet · Plan uses). Under it, one line says what the reason means for this character, so the choice explains itself. The cards' Now and Not now follow it. Opened from the Long Rest window or a level-up card, the reason is already set; opened from Sheet, it starts on After a Long Rest, the usual case, not Just looking.

Board 2 · the step card

The instructions stay on screen

While a run is open, every askdnd page you go to shows the step's card: what to do, what to expect, and Passed, Failed…, Skip. Passed moves on to the next step and opens its page; the last one brings you back to the queue with the run's tally.

The card is yours alone, never in a screenshot it takes for a note, and it never covers what it points you at: — folds it to a pill (Testing · 2 of 3) you can press to bring it back.

Board 3 · Failed…

A failure is a feedback note, already filled in

Failed… opens the feedback window you know (\), with Something's broken picked, the step attached, and the page's picture already taken. You can point at the part as now, or mark the picture: a box, an arrow or a line, then a sentence.

It lands in Feedback like any note, tagged with its step, so I read it with everything else and you follow it in Notes you sent. The step shows Failed · note 81 until it's fixed.

The mark-up is new for every note, not only a test's: it's what you'd want on a phone too, where pointing is hard.

Build: S for the link · S for marking a picture

Board 4b · every note gets its check

A broken note comes with its check, and whoever saw it confirms the fix

When I take up a Something's broken note, I write its check with it (what to do, what you should see), so every fix comes back to be confirmed, never just closed. An idea that's built gets one too.

Your notes land in your Testing queue, as on Board 1. A player's note asks them first: Does it work now? on Notes you sent. Yes closes it (they see Built); Still broken opens it again with their picture. It's in your queue under With a player, so you see the answer, and it comes to you after three days without one (question 6).

Build: S · the check made with the note, Does it work now? on Notes you sent
Board 5 · for your next project

Feedback, Testing, View as and the Mac Mini, as one kit to take along

These are how you and I work, more than they are askdnd: you notice something, send it with a picture, I fix it, the check comes back, you confirm. They're built so they can move to another project with little change:

FeedbackNotes with pictures, kinds, a thread, Notes you sentits own tables and pages; the \ key
TestingChecks, rounds, the step card, does it work now?its own tables; reads Feedback
View asSee any page through someone's eyes, changing nothing for themone rule for what it hides
The Mac MiniRuns with several real seats; nightly checks; reports to Sentryscripts, kept apart from the app

What makes them portable: each keeps to its own folder and tables, and talks to the rest of the app through one small adapter (who the people are, how to switch to one, what the pages are). My command line (feedback.py, and a testing part of it) uses the same keys. When it's built, docs/toolkit.md says how to bring the kit into a new project, step by step, as the setup log does for hosting and domains.

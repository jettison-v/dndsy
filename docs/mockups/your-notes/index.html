One page for what you sent

Today a player has two pages: Your suggestions (corrections and additions to the story, which the DM reads) and Your feedback (notes about askdnd itself, which Brett and then Claude read). This puts them on one page that says plainly which is which, and adds a way to know when something moved.

Layout A. Two tabs

The page opens on whichever tab has news (else the one you used last). Each tab starts with who reads it and how to send one, then your notes, newest first, with their own words for where each stands. A red dot marks what moved since you last looked.

Knowing something moved: three ways

Things that would count as news: the DM added or used your correction, or decided not to; your askdnd note was planned, fixed or closed; a reply to you. Each shows once.

My pick

1. A badge on your picture

● Live?P2

A count on your picture (on More on a phone) and beside Your notes in the menu; it clears when you open the page. The corner card when something moves stays, once.

Good at
  • Adds nothing to a full menu bar.
  • Quiet: news here is rare (a few a week).

My recommendation: Layout A (two tabs) with a badge on your picture.

The tabs make the difference you want players to understand the first thing on the page, and news here is rare, so a badge is enough without adding an icon to the bar. If askdnd later has more to tell people (a turn coming up, a level-up, the DM's replies), the badge's count can move to a bell then, with the same list behind it.

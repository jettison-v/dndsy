Session Story

Last night's session gets a fuller story: the recap drafted after you've answered Claude's questions and settled the suggested entries (you chose A), plus what you asked for this evening. That's a short and a long telling, awards, the session's numbers, keeping the cleaned transcript, and a more dramatic Listen.

Everything here is made up: the table's characters, an invented evening. It lives in two places you know. The first is Manage › Sessions › a session, where you review a draft. The second is Story, where the table reads it.

1 · The order things happen

From the recording to the Story page

Keeping the cleaned transcript

Recommended: keep it

Why we haven't kept it: the rule came from the first week (DECISIONS, Oct 14). The raw recording holds personal conversation, so we promised that only the story is kept. The cleaned transcript is different. The Processor has cut the table talk and you've reviewed every cut, so what's left is the game.

What keeping it gives
  • Draft the recap and Redraft read the real words every time, with no re-sending from the Processor.
  • The full tale and awards are written from what was said, not from a summary of it.
  • New features (awards, scene times) can run on past sessions too.
How it's kept
  • S3 under v2/transcripts/, encrypted, never in the database or git.
  • No page shows it to anyone. Only Claude reads it when drafting.
  • An admin can delete a session's transcript (Manage › Sessions › a session).
Worth knowing
  • It's still everyone's words, by name. The table should know it's kept, so a line in Help › About says so.
  • The raw recording stays on your Mac, as now.

If you'd rather not keep it, A works as you chose: the first read also writes a detailed account of what happened, and the recap step drafts from that.

The awards Claude can give
  • Most Strategic: the plan that shaped a scene or fight
  • Best Decision: a choice that changed where the story went
  • Best Roleplay: in character when it mattered
  • Line of the Night: an in-character line, quoted
  • Clutch Moment: an action or roll when it counted most
  • Unsung Hero: quiet help that made someone else's moment
  • Hardest Luck: when the dice turned (from Table rolls)
Your list from before

We talked about awards in the planning chat, so the repo doesn't have your list. Change, add or remove any in the box at the bottom. Awards go to characters, never to how well someone played.

6 · Listen

A more dramatic narrator

Now 
The narrator of a dark fantasy series reading its "previously on". Speak low, slow and ominous, with weight on names and dramatic pauses between sentences; never cheerful, never rushed.
Proposed 
An epic fantasy trailer narrator. A low, resonant voice with real swing: hushed and close for mystery, building in pace and volume as danger rises, a held breath before a reveal, then the line landing hard. Lean on names and on the last words of a paragraph. Let the opening "Previously on…" hang. Never cheerful, never flat, never rushed.

The same voice (OpenAI's "onyx"), told to act more. A published recap keeps the reading it has until an admin presses Re-record the voice in the player, which costs a few cents. The full tale gets its own Listen.

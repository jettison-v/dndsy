The Roadmap in askdnd

A first version, inside Backstage, for you and Claude only. It records Roadmap items and lets us work them: their order, their steps and whose each step is, the plans building them, and a short log of what changed. Nothing faces players yet: the bigger design (Roadmap: Screens, paused Nov 17) stays for later. It's how Linear's Projects sit above its issues: a project has its own steps and updates, and its issues and cycles do the work. Everything below is clickable.

An item is a piece of the RoadmapIts title, why it matters, Now, Next, Later or Paused, its size, and a status line saying where it stands in a few words, as today's Roadmap does.
Its steps say whose turn it isA checklist, each step yours or Claude's. The first one not done is its next step. A step that's yours shows in For you, under From the Roadmap, until you tick it.
Plans do the buildingAn item's plans are Work's plans (a build's own batch); their stage shows on the item. Shipped items move to Done with the day. Claude adds and updates items with feedback.py item, as it does now.

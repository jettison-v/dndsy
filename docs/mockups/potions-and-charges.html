Step 5 · the last piece of forgotten effects

Potions and charges at hand

Today Battle shows an item only if you pinned it, and nothing there spends it. This puts everything you can use in the column of what it costs, so there's one place to look. Its button opens a small window, like a roll, and that makes the change on your D&D Beyond sheet.

Where you'll find it

  1. Open your character and go to Battle, the first tab.
  2. In the Action and Bonus action columns, under whatever you pinned, an Items group. It fills itself from your sheet: no pinning.
  3. Each item has its button where an attack has its to-hit: Drink, Read or Use. It opens a small window next to it, as a roll does, at the bottom on phones.
  4. Click anywhere else on the row and the side panel opens with everything about it, like every other row on Battle.
  5. Only on Battle, and only your own character can press. The DM and the table see the same rows, as they already see your items on Party.

What I checked

  • A bug on Battle today: every item is listed under Action. In the 2024 rules drinking a potion, or giving one to someone within 5 feet, is a Bonus Action (DMG, Using a Potion). This build fixes it, along with the old name "Use an item". The Action column stays right for scrolls and most other items, and each one's cost comes from its own text.
  • Weapons with charges are missing: Big C's Hammer of Thunderbolts has 5 charges and shows nowhere on Battle, because weapons are skipped. The Items group includes them.
  • D&D Beyond counts a potion two ways: a quantity (×2) and a one-use pip. One of No Name's potions reads "0 of 1" while still on the sheet. Before building I'll use one of Vi's six potions on D&D Beyond and copy exactly what it does, so askdnd counts the same way.
  • Already built, used here: changes through the bridge, the ↻ mark while one is on its way, Table rolls with Apply to, corner cards, and the rule that a change only works while your sheet can be reached.

Decided, so you don't have to

  • The button says what you do: Drink, Read, or Use. Every use has Undo on its corner card.
  • Order in a column: what you pinned first, then Items: healing, other potions, scrolls, then charged items.
  • Items that don't say their cost: they sit in the Action column marked ●, so askdnd isn't guessing. I'll add the real cost in askdnd's own words for the items your table holds, like Soul Coin, so ● stays rare.
  • On phones: the columns are already tabs, so items are in the tab of their cost.
  • Giving a potion: Give rolls and spends the giver's potion, and the roll offers Apply to Cole to Cole's player and the DM, like any other healing roll. It works for an ally at 0.
  • An effect: using a Potion of Water Breathing starts its pill with its time, replacing today's separate "Used it" button.
  • Used up: a one-use item leaves its column. A charged item stays dimmed with when it comes back.
  • Needs attunement: an item you aren't attuned to stays out.
  • In Wild Shape: Items stay, because the rules leave it to the DM what a form's limbs can handle.
  • Pinned items: yours stay where you put them, at the right cost, and don't show twice.

Two questions for you

Settled Oct 30 · Where it goes

In the columns, with a window like a roll (your comment): one place to go to do things, not a new row to look at. What it costs: Trah's Action column gets long, so items sit under what's pinned and your pins never move.

1 · Does the window's Drink heal you?

A, recommended: yes. Drink rolls in Table rolls for all to see, spends the potion and heals you. There's no ruling to make on healing yourself, and Undo is on the card.

B: Apply to on the roll. Drink rolls and spends it, then you press Apply to, the same as a healing spell. One way for everything, one more press in a fight.

Use Drinking above to feel both.

2 · Should anything remind you on your turn?

A, recommended: your Items light up once as your turn begins in a heard fight. Quiet, and you'd see it each turn. Press Your turn begins above.

B: also a corner card when you're under half your Hit Points and carrying healing. Harder to miss, and one more card each turn of a hard fight.

C: nothing. The columns alone, until a playtest asks for more.

Not in this piece

Class resources (Rage, Focus Points) use the same press later, in 1.3. Charges coming back at dawn are part of rests, 1.4. Ammunition, coins, attunement, handing items over and loot stay in 1.5, inventory in play.

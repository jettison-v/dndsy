Step 5 · forgotten effects

Conditions that end

A condition can say when it ends: at the end of someone's next turn, after a minute, or when a save succeeds. askdnd follows the fight's turns by listening to D&D Beyond's VTT as the DM presses Next. When something ends or asks for a save, it reminds the right person. You or the DM still end it.

Where you'll find it
  1. Battle's Status row: each condition's pill says how long it lasts ("ends at the end of your next turn", "9 rounds left", "Wis 13 save at the end of your turns"). A line above the pills shows the round and whose turn it is.
  2. The badges after the name (every character page): a small amber dot on any that ends this turn; point at a badge for when it ends.
  3. Corner cards: "Frightened ends now · End it", or "Wisdom save to end Paralyzed · Roll the save", shown to that character's player and the DM.
  4. + Condition on Battle: a Lasts menu when you start one. Concentration keeps its pill and now counts its rounds by itself.

Starting one, with how long it lasts

+ Condition on Battle

The kinds, in the 2024 rules' words
  • Until it's ended (Prone, Grappled): no reminder; End it when it's over.
  • Until the start or end of someone's turn ("until the end of your next turn"): pick whose. Most say yours or the creature that caused it.
  • Rounds, or 1 minute (10 rounds): counted from the turn it started, round by round.
  • Minutes or hours (1 hour, 8 hours): shown as a time, not counted in rounds; askdnd reminds you after a rest.
  • Until a save (Hold Person): the ability and DC, and when it's rolled (the end of each of your turns, most often). It can also have a longest time, like 1 minute.
  • With Concentration : when the caster is concentrating, it ends when their Concentration does, and askdnd reminds you.

When turns aren't heard

Nobody has the VTT open

The turn line says Turns not heard. Pills show what was set ("until the end of your next turn") but nothing is counted, and no reminder comes. Concentration keeps its +1 for counting rounds by hand.

The VTT has no fight running

No round or turn, so the turn line is hidden. A condition started outside a fight keeps its time ("1 minute") as a plain label.

Someone isn't in the order

A character the DM didn't add to the VTT's initiative has no turn: "end of Trah's next turn" can't be timed, and the pill says so.

For you to decide

1 · When a duration runs out

A reminder card with End it and Keep, or askdnd ends it by itself (and says so)? A reminder keeps the DM ruling, and covers a DM who says it lasts a little longer. My pick: the reminder.

2 · Who sees a reminder

That character's player and the DM, or the player alone? The DM watches every creature's conditions, so the DM seeing them helps them run the fight. A save is rolled by the player either way. My pick: the player and the DM.

3 · Whose turn, shown where

Only on Battle, above the pills, or in the menu bar on every page too? The menu bar is already full. Battle is where you are in a fight. My pick: Battle only.

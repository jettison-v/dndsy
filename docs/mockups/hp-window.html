Hit points in Wild Shape, worked out for you

D&D Beyond doesn't know Cole is a Polar Bear, so every hit is worked out out loud: does the Bear resist it, how much do the form's Temporary Hit Points take, is Moonbeam still up? Here the hit starts where it really starts: the DM rolls Enemy 1's damage (or Big C rolls a heal), and the roll arrives in Table rolls with its amount and type. Apply to Cole opens this window already filled in; askdnd shows its working by the 2024 rules, then makes the result on Cole's D&D Beyond sheet through the bridge. Nobody types a number. These are Cole's real numbers: Druid 6, Circle of the Moon, 51 HP; as a Polar Bear, AC 17, 18 Temporary Hit Points and the Bear's Cold resistance.

Where you'll find it

It starts in Table rolls, the sidebar down the left of every page in Game Mode. When the DM rolls a damage roll (in D&D Beyond's VTT, as today, or in askdnd), it arrives there with its amount and damage type. On Cole's player's screen it has Apply to Cole; the DM's has Apply to… anyone. That opens the hit points window beside Cole's Hit points on his Battle page (the first number in the row under his name), filled in from the roll. The rest of Battle stays as it is.

Where it opens

  • Hit points on Battle, Sheet and Party, and later on a fight screen.
  • A small window by the number, like the roll window; at the bottom on phones (reading only there).
  • Not in a form: the same window without step 3, with your own resistances.

Decided (Oct 28)

  • Who presses Apply: normally the character's own player (Apply to Cole, on their screen); the DM can too, for anyone (Apply to…).
  • The Concentration save is the player's to roll, after being hit: the reminder and its roll button appear on the character's player's screen, also when the DM applied the damage.
  • Damage types: the ones the character resists first, the rest under Other types.
  • Anyone can cross out a step (a ruling), with the reason shown.
  • The change mark: a green glow and the change for two seconds.

First to check when it's built: whether the DM's rolls in D&D Beyond's VTT reach askdnd with their damage type. If the type isn't in the roll, askdnd takes it from the monster's attack by name (a Bite is piercing), and Change fixes it.

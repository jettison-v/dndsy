Which squares a spell's area covers, and who's in them

Where you'll find it: the area tool in the map's strip, and Cast for a spell with an area, which starts the same tool with its shape and size. Try it: pick a shape and size, click a corner of the grid for its point of origin, turn a Cone or Line, and make Cole Large (a Polar Bear) to see an Emanation grow with the space it starts from. Move Cole and the Emanation moves too.

A square counts when the area covers at least half of it (Dungeon Master’s Guide, areas on a grid). The point of origin is a corner where squares meet.
An Emanation extends from the creature’s whole space and moves with it. Its creature isn’t in it unless its creator decides otherwise. A Large creature’s space is 2 by 2 squares, so the same 10 feet covers more.
askdnd lists who’s in it and each one’s save. Who’s actually in, around cover and close calls, is the DM’s ruling.

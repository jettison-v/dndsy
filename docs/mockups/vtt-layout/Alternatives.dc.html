Considered and set aside

Two other ways to lay it out

Where you'll find it: nowhere: these are the layouts I weighed against the frame. Both are common in other VTTs.

Panels floating over a full-screen map

Menu bar
Turn bar
Table rolls
Side panel
Your turn's bar

Good: the most map, and it looks like Baldur's Gate 3, which players may know.

The cost: panels cover tokens, so people keep moving the map or the panels; a token under a panel is easy to miss mid-fight. askdnd's side panel and Table rolls would work differently here than on every other page.

The map, and one column of tabs

Menu bar
Fight · Rolls · You · Clicked

one tab at a time

Good: the simplest to build, close to Owlbear Rodeo and Roll20.

The cost: during a fight you need the order, the rolls and your own numbers at once, and tabs hide all but one. Your turn's actions end up in a tab instead of under the map where the move happens.

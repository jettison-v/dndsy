Prepare spells · its own screen, with the limits

Pick today's spells, see the count, then make them all at once

Where you'll find it: a Prepare spells button at the top of Sheet › Spells, and on the corner card after a Long Rest ("You can change your prepared spells now"). It opens this screen over the page. Ticking a spell changes nothing yet: the count and the list of changes update as you go, and Make these changes sends them to D&D Beyond in one go. The spell's side panel keeps its single Prepare for one quick swap. Try it: tick and untick, go over 10, filter.

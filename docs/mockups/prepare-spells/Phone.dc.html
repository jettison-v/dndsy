What the screen says, by class (2024 rules)
Cleric, DruidAfter a Long Rest, change any of your prepared spells, from your class's spells of a level you have slots for. The screen lists the whole class list.
WizardAfter a Long Rest, change any, from the spells in your spellbook. The screen lists your spellbook; copying a new spell into it is done on D&D Beyond.
Paladin, RangerAfter a Long Rest, replace one spell. The screen says "the rules allow one change" and counts your changes, without stopping a second.
Bard, Sorcerer, WarlockReplace one spell when you gain a level. The button shows in the Level-up companion's card, not after a rest.

Everywhere: the count against your class's number, the always-prepared spells listed but not counted, and going over allowed and said, for the DM to rule.

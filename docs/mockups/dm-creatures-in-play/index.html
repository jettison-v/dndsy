The DM's creatures in play

Two things the DM does every fight that askdnd's table doesn't help with yet: one roll that hits several of the party at once (a Fireball, a breath weapon), and playing a creature's own turn. Both before the table moves to askdnd's VTT.

Option A · a roll that hits several

Apply to everyone in the area

The DM's damage roll with a save offers Apply to everyone in the area, filled from the area on the map (or picked by hand without one). Each player rolls their own save from their Table rolls; the DM's creatures' saves are rolled for the DM in one press. Each line shows the save, half or full, and what each actually takes after their defenses; one press applies them all.

  • A player who hasn't rolled yet holds only their own line; the rest can go at once.
  • Players see their own line; a creature's damage and Hit Points stay the DM's.

Option B · the same, simpler

One at a time

The same roll walks the DM through each target in turn, in the hit points window as it is today: save, half or full, Apply, next. Less to see at once, more presses.

Option C · the creature's turn

The bar under the map becomes the creature's

On a creature's turn the DM's bar shows that creature as a player's shows their own: its actions to roll, its spell slots or recharges, what it's used this turn, how far it's moved, and Next turn. A group of the same creatures goes one after another, each with its own Hit Points.

  • Hidden from players: they see whose turn it is, as today.
  • Recharge (a breath weapon's 5–6) rolls itself at the start of the creature's turn and says so.

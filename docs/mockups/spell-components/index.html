Spell components

Your note (Nov 8): askdnd should say when you don't have a spell's component, and use one up when a spell consumes it. By the 2024 rules a Component Pouch or a Spellcasting Focus covers any Material component without a cost, so the ones that matter are those with a cost (Revivify's diamond worth 300+ GP), and any component at all when you have neither. askdnd says what's missing and never stops a cast; the DM rules.

Option A

Marked where you choose a spell

A spell whose Material component you don't carry has an amber mark saying what it needs, in Battle's spell rows, the Spell Book and Prepare spells. A spell that consumes something you do carry says so in green, so you know it'll be used up.

  • askdnd reads your inventory: a Component Pouch or Focus covers everything without a cost; gems, dust and the like are matched by name and worth (D&D Beyond's cost for them).
  • Hover the mark for the component's whole words.

Option B and C

Said in the Cast window, and used up when cast

B: the Cast window says plainly when you lack the component, and its button becomes Cast anyway: nothing is stopped, the DM rules. C: a component the spell consumes is taken from your inventory on D&D Beyond in the same cast, from wherever you carry it (a bag included), and the corner card's Undo puts it back.

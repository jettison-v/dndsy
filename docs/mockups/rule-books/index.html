Rule books: a panel for each book

Where it lives: Backstage › General › Rule books, the admins' page listing the private books askdnd reads (the Player's Handbook, the Dungeon Master's Guide and the Monster Manual, all 2024). Today it's a table of titles and counts. Below, pressing a book opens a side panel that says what the book is, what askdnd takes from it, where you meet it in askdnd, and which campaigns have it on. Three picks follow.

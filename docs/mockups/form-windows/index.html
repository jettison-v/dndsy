Form windows: spacing and sections

Every window that asks you for something uses one window piece and one field piece since last round (Save in the foot, the name beside the box). This is the next pass you asked for: the spacing inside them, and how a long one is split into sections. Drawn at real size on three of askdnd's own windows: Add a top-up (Backstage › Usage), New campaign (Backstage › Campaigns) and New item (Backstage › Items). Whatever you pick goes into the two pieces, so every form window changes at once.

The spacing, one scale (in scope, no choice needed)

  • Head to body: 4px, not ~50The first field (or the explanation) sits right under the title, as a page's first line sits under its heading.
  • 12px between fields, 24px before a sectionOne gap inside a section, a bigger one with a line where a new section starts. Never a blank gap of its own (New item's 100px goes).
  • Text one sizeThe explanation, the names and the boxes are all 14px; help under a box 12.5px. The title stays the only big line.
  • Buttons their usual sizeSave and Cancel as everywhere else in askdnd (the top-up's were drawn large).
  • A test that measures itThe layout tests open every form window and fail on a gap off the scale, as they fail on a box that spills today.

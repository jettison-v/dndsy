Pages and comments, both places

Two things you asked for. First, a page (a mockup, a plan, a guide) reaches askdnd in seconds, with no pull request, tests or deploy. What makes it harder than it looks

Only I can reach claude.aiaskdnd's server can't read or write claude.ai: only a Claude session can, through its own tools. So the two merge when I check in, not the instant you press. While I'm listening, I check claude.ai at least every 10 minutes, so it's within 10 minutes; Send to Claude on a claude.ai comment wakes me at once. With no session open, claude.ai's side waits for the next one.
I can't start a claude.ai commentI can read claude.ai comments and reply to ones you've sent to me, but I can't open a new comment thread there. So an askdnd comment shows up inside the page on claude.ai, drawn by the page itself as a pin marked "in askdnd", rather than as a claude.ai comment.
Pins sit differentlyclaude.ai pins a comment to an element on the page. askdnd pins it to a spot, plus the words nearest it. Moving a comment across, I place it by those words, so it lands next to the right thing, though not always on the exact pixel.
Two answers to one questionIf you pick in both places, the newest wins, both places show it, and askdnd keeps both in its history, so nothing is lost. A claude.ai pick answers askdnd's question the same way, marked "on claude.ai".
Not fragile in the bad wayNothing breaks if a check-in is missed: each one compares the full lists on both sides and copies what's missing, so a late one catches up. Every comment carries where it came from, so nothing is copied twice or bounces back and forth.
Small to buildOne askdnd call to receive a page; one to add a comment or pick made elsewhere; the page's picks block taught to show pins from askdnd; and a check-in step in my routine. No new service and no new key.

Three ways to do it

my pick

A · Both ways, merged at each check-in

  • What you asked for: comment and pick in either place, and both show everything.
  • claude.ai comments and picks come into askdnd; askdnd's show inside the page on claude.ai.
  • Merged when I check in: at least every 10 minutes while I'm listening, or at once with Send to Claude.
  • askdnd keeps the record, with where each one came from.

B · Into askdnd only

  • claude.ai comments and picks come into askdnd at each check-in.
  • askdnd's never show on claude.ai, so the claude.ai copy falls behind.
  • A little less to build: the page doesn't draw askdnd's pins.

C · askdnd only

  • Scope v6 as written: you comment in askdnd; claude.ai only if you ask for a page there.
  • The simplest, with nothing to merge.
  • Loses what you asked for: commenting wherever you happen to be.
Why A

The one real limit, that it merges when I check in rather than instantly, is the same for A and B. A adds only the page drawing askdnd's pins, which is small, and it's what makes either place complete on its own. Its fragile spots are contained. A missed check-in is caught up by the next one, and every copy carries its origin, so nothing doubles. If it turns out noisy in use, dropping to B is just turning off the pins on claude.ai.

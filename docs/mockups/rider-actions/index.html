Riders on a Hit

What you can add once an attack hits, as things to press rather than words, using the card you already have. Each rider is one of four kinds, says what it costs, and goes into the same Roll damage. Both cards below are playable: press a rider, then Roll damage.

WhereThe roll card's result, after Roll to hit, under On a hit: the place Savage Attacker and Stunning Strike are written today.
What staysThe steps, the pills, Roll damage, Missed, and Guided Strike and Favored by the Gods under the to-hit. Only the riders change.
Where the data livesEach rider already comes from askdnd's class rules; it gains a kind, a cost and its numbers, so the card plays it like everything else.

Every rider says what it costs (a Focus Point, a slot, a use, once per turn). Where the D&D Beyond sheet can't be reached, the cost line says so ("mark the Focus Point on D&D Beyond") and the dice or the save still go to Table rolls. The every-button test presses each rider once per character, as it does Guided Strike now.

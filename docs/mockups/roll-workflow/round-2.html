The roll card, to play with

Press the +7 or Cast on Battle and play the whole action through, the way you would at the table. Each of the three actions follows its own path. The dice are real (random), the numbers are the characters' own, and Table rolls fills in on the left as it would at the table, and below the screen is what askdnd keeps of the roll as you go.

What askdnd keeps of this roll

Kept with every roll, so later it can answer: the night's best and worst luck, damage by person and by kind, how often Stunning Strike landed, which slots went to Smites, every Grapple and who escaped. Session Story's Hardest Luck and Damage King read this today; this keeps more of the flavor.

Next d20:

A 20 shows the critical: damage dice doubled, a Smite's too. A low one lets you try Big C's Favored by the Gods after a miss, or No Name's Heroic Inspiration reroll.

What's decided here, and what isn't yet

  • Hit or miss is the DM's call, said by the player: Roll damage means "it hits", Missed ends it. askdnd never assumes a target's Armor Class.
  • Riders sit where the rules put them: what adds to the d20 before the roll (Bless, a Luck Point), what's chosen once it hits (Hex, Stunning Strike, Divine Smite), what comes after a miss (Favored by the Gods).
  • Spending is part of the roll: a Focus Point, a slot, a Luck Point or Inspiration is spent when the roll is, through the bridge, and Undo puts it back.
  • A check or save is the short version: the same card with only its mode and pills, one Roll, done.
  • On a laptop the card opens beside the row you pressed, so Battle and Table rolls stay in view; on a phone the same card rises from the bottom, within thumb reach.
  • Not drawn here: the DM's side of a save, and askdnd's own table with its map.

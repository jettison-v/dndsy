Conditions on Battle

Your idea (Oct 28): today a condition is only a pill under Battle's numbers, easy to scroll past. Here the page itself shows it: a halo around the edges, and an emblem in the corner for each condition, with its own colour and icon. Switch conditions on below and try the treatments.

Where you'll find it
Halo: the page's edges Emblems Dice tray
  1. On your character's Battle page, and on anyone's screen looking at it: the DM opening Cole's Battle sees what Cole has.
  2. The halo is the page's own edge, so it shows whatever you're scrolled to.
  3. The emblems sit in the bottom-right corner, the one that's free now that the D&D Beyond pill is gone for players. Bottom-left stays the dice tray; on a phone they sit just above the bottom bar.
  4. The pills under the numbers stay: they're where you start and end a condition and read it in full.
  5. On Party, each card shows the same icons small.

Point at an emblem (tap on a phone) for what it does. Starting a condition makes the edge flash once and the emblem pop in; after that nothing moves, so it never pulls your eye in the middle of a turn. The disadvantage and advantage marks on the numbers are askdnd's today, unchanged.

The colours and icons

Each condition has its own colour and icon, drawn for askdnd (D&D Beyond's icons are its own art, so I haven't copied them). They fall in three families, which is what the halo shows with How serious: a red edge says you can't act, amber that you're hindered, blue that something's helping.

Signature touches

Where a condition changes how the page feels

  • Invisible: your portrait goes faint with a dashed outline.
  • Unconscious and Blinded: the page darkens towards its edges.
  • Prone: its emblem leans.
  • Exhaustion: its level on the emblem.

Only where it says something about the rule; the rest stay plain so the page doesn't turn into a light show.

What stays the same

  • Starting and ending a condition: its pill, or D&D Beyond's Conditions (the bridge brings it either way).
  • Everyone at the table sees them, as today.
  • askdnd shows what a condition does; the DM rules on it.
Your call
  1. The page: halo, tint, both, or emblems only? I'd go with the halo: it's there on every scroll without changing the numbers' colours. A tint reads as "the page looks off" more than as a condition.
  2. The halo's colour: how serious, or each condition's own? I'd go with how serious: one glance says "can't act" or "hindered", and the emblems carry each condition's own colour. Several own colours at once blur into brown.
  3. Signature touches: keep them? I'd keep these four and add no more.
  4. Beyond Battle: your character's button in the menu bar also gets a dot per condition (shown above), so you see it on Story or the Spell Book. Keep, or Battle only?
  5. Concentration: it's already a pill in the header. Give it an emblem too? I'd leave it as the pill: it's not a condition, and the hit points window already reminds you of its save.

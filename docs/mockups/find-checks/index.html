Finding checks, skills and saves

Players couldn't find Checks, skills, saves, Death save, Hit Die: it's a fold at the very bottom of Table rolls, under the rolls. Four ways to make it easy, drawn at laptop size with Big C's real numbers. Each one is playable: press things. D can go with any of the others.

My pick, with D

A. A Roll button at the top

One button beside the Table rolls title, always in view. It opens a window with a search box (type ste for Stealth) and everything you can roll that isn't on Battle's columns: checks, saves, skills, Death save, Hit Die and a manual roll. Choosing one opens the roll card for it.

One Picture window, wherever a picture goes

Brett, Nov 4: describe a picture and have it drawn, in one style per campaign, as well as uploading one; and one way of adding pictures and one place to keep them, so askdnd stays simpler. Today there are two ways in: a World entry's Add a picture (the DM's, a file button) and the feedback window's pasted screenshot. Both already keep files the same way (resized, in askdnd's file storage). Proposed: every picture slot opens the same window (board 2), and every picture is kept as one kind of thing (board 4).

Where a picture goes

PA familiar or companionPip: its half of Trah's Party card, its Battle band, the fight band, Table rolls
MA World entryA person, place or faction (today's Add a picture), a map, a banner
+ PictureAny of them with none yetThe same empty slot everywhere; pressing it opens the Picture window

A player character keeps its D&D Beyond portrait, as now. A companion's picture is its World entry's picture (round 2's slice 9), so there's one picture, wherever it shows.

Three ways to give it one, in one window

UploadDrop, paste or choose a picture; on a phone, the camera roll
Describe itA few words, drawn in the campaign's art style (board 3); pick one of four
From D&D BeyondIts stat block's art, shown from D&D Beyond (a link, never copied)

Then the same last step for all three: a square crop for the round picture, and Use this picture. Changing or removing it is the same window.

What stays the same everywhere

One window

The same buttons, words and steps for a familiar, an NPC or a map.

One kind of thing kept

A picture: its file, its square version, where it came from (uploaded, drawn from a description, D&D Beyond).

One place

askdnd's file storage, as today: on beta, S3 under v2/files/, by campaign.

One rule for who

Whoever can change the thing can change its picture: the DM for the World, a player for their own familiar.

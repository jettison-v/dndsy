4 · Where it's kept · Brett chose all three (Nov 4)

One kind of picture, one place, one rule for who

Built on what askdnd already does for a World entry's picture (resized to WebP, with a square version; camera details like location removed), so nothing new to keep safe or back up.

What's kept for each picture

  • The file: up to 1200 px, and a 320 px square for round places (Party, the fight band, Table rolls)
  • Where it lives: askdnd's file storage, as today: S3 under v2/files/, by campaign; backed up nightly with the database's list of files
  • Where it came from: uploaded, drawn from a description (with its words and the style it was drawn in), or a link to D&D Beyond's art
  • Whose it is: a World entry (a person, place, faction, map) or a creature on a character's sheet; a companion's is its World entry's
  • Who added it, and when, in the campaign's History, with Restore

Who may change it

  • A World entry's: the DM, reviewers and admins (as today)
  • A familiar's or companion's: its master's player and the DM
  • The campaign's art style: the DM and admins
  • Drawing from a description: the same people, while the campaign has it on and the monthly limit (Manage › Usage) has room

Everyone at the table sees every picture, like the rest of the World.

Decided

Who draws them

Chosen: OpenAI
  • OpenAI's GPT Image: follows a description closely and gives the most alike results run to run; takes up to 16 pictures to match
  • Google's Gemini image: as good at keeping one character the same, up to 14 pictures to match
  • Either needs its own key (scripts/set-secret.sh), on the stack map; Claude doesn't draw pictures

How many per press

Chosen: four
  • Four to pick from costs four times one, still a few cents
  • One is cheaper but more often needs another press

Players drawing

Chosen: yes
  • A player draws their own familiar's picture; the DM can switch Describe it off for the campaign
  • Or the DM only, with players uploading

Order of work: the one window with Upload and From D&D Beyond first (familiars and World entries; no new service), then Describe it and the art style once you've picked who draws them and added its key.

3 · The campaign's art style

One style per campaign, so its pictures belong together

Where you'll find it: Manage › Settings › Art style, the DM's or an admin's. Every picture drawn from a description in this campaign gets it: a few words, plus up to four of the campaign's own pictures to match. Uploads stay as they are.

What's sent to draw a picture

The words for that picture, the style's words and its pictures to match. Never a transcript, notes or anything else of the campaign.

A new style later

Pictures drawn before keep their look. Each keeps its own words, so a DM who changes the style can redraw one (or all of them) in the new one, and pick again.

Off for a campaign

With no style saved, Describe it still works with a plain default style; the DM can switch Describe it off for the campaign, leaving Upload and D&D Beyond.

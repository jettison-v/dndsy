The component library · step 6

One Use window for what a feature spends

Your question on the Roll Audit (Nov 12): how would Lay on Hands work? Today it takes two windows: Resources' Use one (How many) and then the hit points window. This is one window that spends, picks who, and applies, made from the feature's data, so Font of Magic, Second Wind and the rest come free.

Where it livesA feature's row on Battle (its column by cost: Lay on Hands under Bonus Action), Search, and the Resources row's count. The same window, whichever opens it.
Who uses itThe character's player, and the DM for anyone. What it changes goes to D&D Beyond through the bridge, with Undo, like Cast.
How to try it locallyOnce built, a local test table with Big C and Trah (your ask), so you can heal, spend and undo without touching a real sheet.

Option A · recommended

Spend, pick who, apply: one press

How many starts at what fills the one you pick ("All it needs"), never more than the pool. One press spends the points on D&D Beyond, heals Cole through the hit points window's own rules (Temporary Hit Points untouched, Unconscious ends at 0), and leaves an entry in Table rolls with a corner card and Undo for both. End Poisoned spends 5 and ends the condition.

What makes it general

The feature's data says what the window asks

Each feature's entry in the rules data gets a use: what it spends (a pool, a count, a slot), who it's for (you, one creature, creatures in an area), and its ways (heal, a roll, end a condition, convert). The window draws only what's there: Second Wind has no For (it's you) and rolls its dice; Font of Magic has two ways and no target. The same rule as the roll card: the data decides, the window plays it.

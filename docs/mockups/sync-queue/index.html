The Sync queue

You asked for “visibility into the askdnd sync… something like a processing queue”, to look into problems and anything missed. Here's what it would be: one page in Backstage listing every piece of work between askdnd and D&D Beyond, what's waiting, running, done or failed, and the story of each one when you open it.

Where it lives, and what it shows

WhereBackstage › askdnd › Sync, beside the D&D Beyond bridge page. The bridge page keeps its setup and health; this one is the work itself. Admins only.
Reads ← D&D Beyondaskdnd fetching a sheet: a page opened, Refresh now, a fight, or the every-few-minutes look at sheets nobody has open. Tonight's jobs work (Roadmap item 43) moves these onto one queue, which is what makes this page possible.
Changes → D&D BeyondWhat a person did in askdnd that D&D Beyond should hold (hit points, a spell slot, a potion), sent through the player's own tab by the extension and read back. Today these show only in the bridge's log.
Why it helpsWhen a number looks wrong at the table, you can see whether askdnd ever asked, whether D&D Beyond answered, how many tries it took, and the exact error, without asking me to dig.

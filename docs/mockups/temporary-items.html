Step 5 · everything that pops up

Corner cards and badges

About ten things pop up today, in five places. This puts anything you read and dismiss in one place, a corner card at the bottom right, and moves the conditions next to the character's name on every character page. Battle keeps its coloured edge, and the dice stay where they are.

Where you'll find it
① badges by the name ② corner cards ③ others' dice (stays) Table rolls
  1. Badges after the name in the header of Battle, Sheet and Plan: small, in each condition's own colour and icon, Concentration first. Hover or tap one for what it does. They leave Battle's bottom-right corner.
  2. Corner cards, bottom right, stacked with the newest at the bottom: "Saved", errors, a refused button's reason, someone's roll when your dice animation is off, Keep going. On a phone they sit just above Table rolls' bar, one at a time.
  3. Unchanged: others' dice in the tray beside Table rolls, your own dice across the page, Battle's coloured edge, the Status row's pills, and the ↻ mark and change glow on a number.

The kinds of card

✓Done"Saved", "Wild Shape: Polar Bear". Fades after 4 seconds.
iFor your informationGame Mode started or ends soon, someone's roll for the DM. 6 seconds.
20Someone's rollOnly when your dice animation is off: the die and the total, as the corner note shows it today. 6 seconds.
!RefusedWhy a button didn't work ("Cole's sheet isn't open"). 10 seconds.
✕ErrorSomething didn't save. Stays until you close it.

Every card has ✕, and at most one button (Undo, Keep going, Try again); a card with a button stays 20 seconds. Three at most on a computer, the oldest leaving first; one on a phone.

For you to decide

1 · The badges: icons only, or with names?

Switch Badges above to compare. Icons only keeps the header one line on a phone; the name is a hover or tap away, and the Status row on Battle already names each one. My pick: icons only.

2 · Someone's roll with your animation off

Today the note sits bottom left, where their dice would land. As a corner card it joins everything else at the bottom right, so someone's roll shows in one of two corners depending on your animation setting. My pick: the corner card, as you suggested: the tray is the dice, the card is a message.

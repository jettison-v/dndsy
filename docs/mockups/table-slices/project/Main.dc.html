Built since Nov 2 that the table builds on

  • Initiative from askdnd: players roll from Battle; the DM orders the fight on Party's Rows.
  • Creatures in the fight: familiars, summons and steeds take turns beside their master.
  • Hide and Ready on Battle, Wild Shape forms, conditions that end on a turn.
  • Your creatures and the Monster Manual as a book: the stat blocks slice 3 shows.
  • Pictures: one per character and creature, the tokens' faces in slice 6.
  • Staging: several seats and phones at once, before a slice reaches your table.

How each slice reaches the table: you try it locally; staging when it needs several people or a phone; then main, in Labs for VTT test, then your campaign when you switch it on.

Questions for you, as we walk through

  1. The order above? I recommend it.

  4. A group of the same creature: one Initiative roll for the group, the DMG's way, or one each? I recommend one for the group, kept as a battle setting (slice 7).

What the icons say

Shape and colour both say it (a circle with a tick, a triangle), so it reads without colour. The rules let anyone pick up anything; "a catch" means a cost the rules attach, never "can't".

Hovering the triangle on Chain Mail: Not trained: Heavy Armor. The details say the rest (AC, Strength, Stealth, spellcasting).

Simple VTT

What "simple" could cover, so a fight can run in askdnd instead of D&D Beyond's VTT. My recommendation is at the end of each section. Press Next turn, click a token, and switch between the DM and a player below.

Where you'll find it

A new Table item in the menu bar, between Campaign and your character, at /c/<campaign>/table. It's hidden behind a VTT switch until you're happy with it, so the test campaign sees it first. The fight band, Table rolls, corner cards, the hit points window and the roll window all stay as they are: this page brings them together around a board.

What simple means

In
  • A fight askdnd runs itself: turn order, whose turn, rounds, Next turn
  • Enemies from the rules data, each with their own Hit Points
  • One map: an image the DM uploads, with a square grid
  • Tokens for characters and enemies, dragged by whoever owns them, seen moving by everyone at once
  • Everything askdnd already does: rolls, conditions, Hit Points, reminders
Left out for now
  • Fog of war, walls and lines of sight
  • Measuring and spell areas
  • Attacks that work out their own results: the DM still rules, and Apply to stays
  • Several scenes, music and handouts
  • Moving on a phone: phones follow along and use their sheet

The order I'd build it

Each step is useful alone, and the table can stop at any of them. D&D Beyond's VTT keeps working throughout: askdnd goes on listening to it until a fight starts in askdnd instead.

1

The board without a map

Turn order, enemies with Hit Points, and everyone's numbers as one row each, in place of the map. This also gives the DM the compact fight view that's missing today.

For you to decide

1. Is a fight without a map worth having first?

The board in step 1 can ship in about a week and works alongside D&D Beyond's map. Going straight to the map takes longer but gets you off D&D Beyond sooner.

My pick: the board first. It's useful at the very next session.

2. Who moves what?

Players move their own token and the DM moves anything. Or only the DM moves tokens, which is simpler but slower at the table.

My pick: players move their own.

3. What do players see of an enemy?

Nothing but the token, a rough state (Healthy, Bloodied, Down), or exact Hit Points.

My pick: rough state, with a DM setting. Bloodied is half Hit Points or less.

4. Where do rolls go once askdnd runs the fight?

The Game Log line has no place when no one's on D&D Beyond's VTT. Table rolls alone would be enough.

My pick: Table rolls alone in an askdnd fight, as the Table Without Modes design already expects.

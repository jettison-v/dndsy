The Work Pages

Rebuilt from the start, in Backstage mode. Three pages, each with one job, and one window for any single item. It's the shape the tools people use for this work share: Linear and GitHub have one inbox of what needs you, and Productboard and Linear sort what comes in before it becomes work. A Linear project owns its issues, and a TestRail run tests one release. Linear and GitHub open any one item in a panel over the list. Testing stops being a page: each plan carries its own test round. Everything below is clickable.

For you your inbox · where the day starts

"What do I need to do?"
  • Only what waits on you, grouped by what you do: Try, Decide, Sort
  • Every row says why it's there
  • Done leaves the list; the count on Backstage is the rows here

Work the queue · what's being built

"What's happening, and what's next?"
  • One row per plan, in your order, with the line Claude works down to
  • Each row: stage, issues fixed, checks passed, who it waits on
  • Tabs: Queue · Done · Checks · Scheduled · How we work

A plan opened from Work

"Where is this piece of work?"
  • Owns everything of its batch: Overview · Issues · Test round · Timeline
  • The test round is its checks × your devices, made when it goes live
  • A failed check files an issue back into the plan

Feedback what came in

"What have people found?"
  • To sort: a note is filed, joined to an issue, or declined with why
  • Issues: every issue, with views (Open, Ready to try, Not in a plan, From checks, Closed)
  • Ideas: enhancements, until one joins the Roadmap
One item, one windowAn issue, a check, a note or a question opens in the same window over the page, from anywhere. You act in it, and ↑ ↓ move through the list it came from. A plan is a group, so it opens its page.
Every row says whyOn For you, the reason it waits on you ("Live since 08:40, your try", "Claude asks"). On Work, who it waits on. Short titles, the part of askdnd first.
One plan per Product AreaDone on askdnd.ai (Nov 20): the two Manage plans and Item Builder are one Manage plan, "Found, Fixed, Tried" and "Working with Claude" joined Feedback and Testing, five finished plans went to Done, and all 28 open issues have short titles.

What this is for your goal, Nov 20

An internal operations tool for building and running software: what people find, the work it becomes, its tests and its release, with Claude as the developer. askdnd is its first user and its test ground; the pages and words stay free of D&D, so it can serve another product or company. So it follows what established tools do (an inbox of what needs you, triage, a plan that owns its tests, an item's history), sized for a small team.

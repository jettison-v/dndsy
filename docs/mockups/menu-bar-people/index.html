4 · As the window narrows

What gives way first, and in what order

Your note: the word "askdnd" should be the first thing to go. Here is the full order, drawn on the fullest bar there is: an admin who also plays, with the Live pill's longest words. Each step happens only below its width, and the logo and Search's ⌘F stay at every laptop width. Each bar measures itself.

  1. 1 · 1360pxThe word "askdnd" goes; its logo stays and still opens the campaign. Most laptops are narrower than 1360px, so most people never see the word.
  2. 2 · 1240pxSearch's bar becomes its button: the magnifier and ⌘F, as today. Until then it takes only the room the bar has free, from 380px down to 160px, so it's always the first thing to shrink (you chose D, Nov 13).
  3. 3 · 1200pxYour character's name goes, leaving their picture (pointing at it says the name). Party ▾ keeps its word.
  4. 4 · 1100pxThe Live pill says only what to do: "Via the DM · open your sheet" becomes "Open your sheet", "D&D Beyond not answering" becomes "Not answering", "3 of 4 · 1 via you" becomes "3 of 4". Its colour still says which state it is, and the Live window has the rest. It's never a bare "Live".
  5. 5 · 1000pxThe campaign's name shortens with "…", down to about 120px. Pointing at it shows the whole name. Until here it's always whole: it's the last thing to give way.
  6. NeverThe logo, Campaign, Table, Party ▾, the Live pill (its short words from step 4 on), Manage ▾, Search with ⌘F (a bar, then a button) and your picture all stay at every laptop width.
  7. 900pxThe phone layout, as today: the bar keeps the logo, the campaign's name and the Live pill, and the bottom bar takes Campaign, your character, Party and More.

7 · Search

Five ways to draw Search, each keeping ⌘F in sight

Your note: a few different styles for Search. Each is drawn on the two bars that matter: a player's (the most room) and an admin who plays with the Live pill's longest words (the least). Whichever you pick, ⌘F, Ctrl F and ⌘K open it from any page, and the box it opens is the same: everything you can find, matched as you type.

Steeds, summons and Wild Companion: who keeps them

Where you'll find them: the same places as Pip and Vi's Homunculus today: the switch by the name on Battle (Cole · Bestial Spirit), its own half of its master's Party card, the fight's rows and band, Table rolls. What's new is what brings each one, what sends it away, and where its numbers come from. Checked on Levi's test seat (Nov 4): D&D Beyond's Manage Extras has the familiar forms and ordinary monsters, but no 2024 summon spirit (Bestial Spirit, Draconic Spirit…) and no Otherworldly Steed.

A · askdnd keeps summons and steeds

Recommended
  • Like a Wild Shape form: the creature is askdnd's, so it works whether or not the sheet is open.
  • Its Hit Points, damage and healing are kept in askdnd (− and + on its Battle, Apply to from Table rolls), and everyone at the table sees them.
  • Nothing is written to D&D Beyond for it; the spell slot and Concentration still are, as today.
  • Its numbers change by themselves when it's cast at another level.

B · A homebrew creature on D&D Beyond for each

  • The DM makes each spirit and steed as homebrew on D&D Beyond, at one fixed level; askdnd adds it to Extras when cast.
  • D&D Beyond would hold its Hit Points, but not the numbers at another level: a new homebrew per level.
  • More work for the DM, for numbers askdnd already works out.

Where the numbers come from

Each spirit's and steed's stat block is in the 2024 Player's Handbook, not the free rules. askdnd reads them like the Monster Manual: from the DM's own copy, kept only in askdnd's database, never in the code, listed as one of the campaign's books (Manage › Settings › Books, "PHB 2024"). Without it, a summon shows what the free rules give and says the rest is the DM's.

The same everywhere else

One at a time where the spell says so. A corner card with Undo whenever one comes or goes. Nothing ends by itself except where the rules say so outright (0 Hit Points, Concentration ending, a Long Rest for Wild Companion), and each of those can be put back on the DM's ruling.

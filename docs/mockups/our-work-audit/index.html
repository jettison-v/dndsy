Our Work, page by page

I walked every Our Work page on askdnd.ai (For you, Feedback, the Roadmap and an item, Development's Board, Your order, Checks and a plan, Documents) and every move between them: what a click does, and whether it keeps you where you were. The pattern you asked about is real: lists like For you, Feedback and Checks open things in the item window beside the list, but the Board, Your order and the Roadmap send you to a new page. And links inside the window change page too. Nothing below changes until you pick.

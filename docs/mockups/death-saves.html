Death Saves

At 0 Hit Points, askdnd keeps the death save marks where your hit points were, reminds you when your turn comes round (it hears the turns from D&D Beyond's VTT), and points out massive damage to the DM. You roll; the DM rules. askdnd never decides that someone dies.

Made-up numbers. Trah has 31 Hit Points at most. The marks are D&D Beyond's own: askdnd shows and makes them on Trah's sheet through the bridge, as it already does for hit points.

Where you'll find it
  1. Battle and Sheet, the HP tile. At 0 it becomes the Dying tile: 0 / 31, three success marks, three failure marks and the Death save roll. It goes back to HP as soon as Trah has Hit Points again.
  2. Corner cards, bottom right. When Trah's turn starts: a card for Trah's player with Roll. The DM gets one to know. No one else does.
  3. The fight band and Party. Everyone sees Trah's token striped with "0 HP", and on Party, Trah's marks and how others can help. Hit points are already for the whole table.
  4. The hit points window. Damage that drops Trah to 0, damage at 0, healing at 0, and massive damage each show in its working, step by step.

What askdnd never does

  • Decide a character is dead. Three failures or massive damage show and are told to the DM; the DM rules.
  • Roll for anyone. The card offers Roll; the player rolls when they're ready.
  • Wait for the VTT. Without turns heard, the Dying tile keeps its Roll button and there's simply no card.
  • Track a monster's. Monsters usually just fall at 0; the DM's own call.

Your call

1 · A death save rolled from the Dying tile

A · recommendedIt marks itself: 10 or more a success, under 10 a failure, a 1 two failures, a 20 back to 1 Hit Point. The player pressed Roll for it, and the rule says what it counts as.
BThe roll comes with Mark success (or failure) in Table rolls; nothing changes until someone presses it.

Either way, a death save rolled on D&D Beyond's sheet marks there, and askdnd hears it.

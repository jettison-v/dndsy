Session 19 · The sealed letter

The party left the Saltgate at first light, the harbourmaster's sealed letter wrapped in oilcloth and tucked inside Cole's coat. The northern road was quiet until the bridge, where the river had risen in the night.

They crossed by the old ford instead, and on the far bank found a camp abandoned in a hurry: the fire still warm, a map pinned under a stone, and tracks leading up into the hills.

Big C kept the map. No Name kept watch. Nobody slept well.

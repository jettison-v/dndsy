Items that bring a creature

Trah's Beasty Bone is a homebrew item the DM wrote. Thrown as an Action, it becomes an undead version of the beast the bone came from: immune to poison, with its own turn, commanded with a Bonus Action within 60 ft. It lasts up to an hour or until 0 Hit Points, then it's spent until a Long Rest. Today askdnd shows it only as words. Here it plays like a familiar. The same flow fits the book's items that bring a creature (a Figurine of Wondrous Power, a Bag of Tricks), which would come set up by askdnd.

The beast here is the SRD's Wolf. The item doesn't say which beast, so that's the DM's call.

Two choices for you

1. Who sets it up · my pick: AA homebrew item's words can't say everything (which beast?), so someone tells askdnd once. A, the DM in Correct this, with askdnd's reading of the words filled in to check. B, its player, which the DM can change. C, askdnd's reading alone, which stalls on "which beast".
2. On D&D Beyond · my pick: AA, askdnd's own creature, like Find Steed's: undead and its immunities show as they are, Hit Points kept in askdnd. B, also added to D&D Beyond's Extras as the plain Wolf (Hit Points kept there, like Cole's familiar), with the undead changes only in askdnd.

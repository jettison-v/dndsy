2Recap

This is what the table reads on Previously on…

Beneath the flooded chapel at Saltmere, the party found the Drowned Bell still ringing, its sound pulling sailors into the sea. Wren recovered the tide-key from the bell's clapper while Tobin held back the drowned acolytes with a wall of fire.

The bard Tamsin Vell revealed she had been sent by the Harbor Guild to silence the bell, not to help the town. The party chose to let her leave, but kept her map of the sea caves.

The session ended on the chapel steps at dawn, the bell finally quiet, with no word yet from the harbor master and one acolyte unaccounted for.

A shorter Manage menu

The plan stands: a short menu, a list down the left of every Manage page, and an Overview for askdnd. Round 2 is about one thing it didn't make clear: which half of Manage you're in (this campaign, or askdnd as a whole, the same for every campaign), and how to get to the other half, or to another campaign.

Round 2 · This campaign or askdnd: three ways

Each option shows the menu, then the top of a Manage page. The campaign half is red, askdnd's half is blue in all three, so the colour alone tells you where you are. Switching campaigns works the same in each: the campaign name at the top left (it keeps you on the same Manage page), and the campaign's own menu lists your other campaigns.

A

Two menus: Manage for the campaign, Admin for askdnd

Recommended

Two places with two names. Manage ▾ is always this campaign's (what the DM and reviewers use); Admin ▾, with a gear, is askdnd as a whole and only admins see it. You never have to work out which half you're in: the menu you opened says so, and the page carries a band in that menu's colour.

A shorter Manage menu

The menu keeps only what a DM or admin opens every week. Everything else is one click further, in a section list down the left of every Manage page, so nothing wraps and nothing is lost. Each page keeps its own address.

1 · The menu: today and proposed

Today the menu lists all 17 pages an admin can open. The proposal shows four campaign pages with counts, and one way into the rest.

2 · Every Manage page: a section list down the left

Instead of a row of tabs that wraps onto two lines, each Manage page lists its group's pages down the left, in three small groups. Separate pages, not one long page to scroll: Sessions and History are big on their own, and each page keeps the address you can bookmark.

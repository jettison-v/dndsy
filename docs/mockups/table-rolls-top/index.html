Table Rolls' Top

Since tonight, Table rolls runs the full height of the window on a laptop, beside the menu bar. Its top row still looks off: the red line under the menu bar runs across it as if it were part of the bar, while the line between them says they're separate. Three ways apps usually settle this, drawn in askdnd's colours.

Where this is: every page in a campaign, on a laptop (1241px wide or more) with Table rolls open. Phones and narrower windows aren't affected: there Table rolls opens over the page, below the menu bar.

A · The panel is its own column Recommended

Table rolls is one column from top to bottom, with a thin line under its title. The red line belongs to the menu bar alone and stops at the panel's edge. The usual pattern for a full-height panel (Slack, Notion, VS Code): the eye reads two clear pieces, the page with its bar, and the panel beside it.

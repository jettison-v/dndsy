Readers, built for the job

Your note on Testing's Reader (Nov 17): it's confusing, hard to read and complicated, because it borrowed Feedback's parts instead of being built for testing. This draws both again from what each one is for. Testing answers one question: what do I do with this check now? Feedback answers another: what did they see, and where does it stand? They share a few new pieces, added to the design system, and each is laid out for its own job.

Where they liveManage › askdnd › Testing (For you) and Manage › askdnd › Feedback, as today. Admins only, so new pieces in the design system are fine (your note).
What's wrong today
  • A status line that mixes two things: "Failed · Testing · Manage" is the state and the page it's about.
  • The title three times, six headings that all look alike, and a history whose Failed is a green dot.
  • Four equal buttons: Passed, Failed…, + Issue and an unclear Try it again. Feedback is an accordion of long lines.

Board 1 · Testing, For you

The check you're on: what it is, where it stands, and the one thing to do next

Top to bottom, the order you need it in. The title, once. Where it stands in one sentence, its state as a word in its colour (no dots to decode, no key). One main button for what comes next, here Try it: its page with the card. Marking it sits beside, as one small set: Passed, Failed…, + Note. Then the walkthrough exactly as the card shows it, so you know what you'll see on the page. Why it's here and its history are one line each, opened when you want them.

Board 3 · Feedback, rebuilt

A note: what they saw, what they said, the conversation, and where it stands

Feedback is a different job: reading what someone found and moving it along. So the same list on the left as Testing (kind and state as words, newest first), and on the right the evidence first: their picture, large, with their marks, and the page's facts beside it (where, when, the browser, what they pointed at). Then the conversation as a conversation: their words, Claude's replies and yours as messages, with one box to answer, to Claude or to them. Where it stands is the same header as a check, its next step the main button.

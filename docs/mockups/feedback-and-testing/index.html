Found, fixed, tried

How what anyone finds, what I fix and what you try fit together: one model, one home and one set of words for each thing, built to lift out of askdnd for another project or a company.

What v2 changes, and why

You agreed v1 (every pick A). The devil's-advocate audit found it right in direction but not ready to build. v2 builds every finding in, so the screens and the rebuild start from something complete:

  • Three more things in the model: a try (who, on what device, on which deployed version), a check's versions, and questions and decisions of their own (section 3).
  • One table of every state change, so no screen can contradict another (section 5).
  • A trust and privacy layer, so a player's words, emails and private pages never reach me wrongly, and nothing anyone writes is read as an instruction (section 6).
  • Every workflow end to end, including the twelve the audit found missing (section 7).
  • Whose turn it is as the one colour rule, and far fewer words (section 4).
  • Cut: two-way sync with Jira; deferred: an area in a chat of its own until its groundwork exists (section 9).

2 · What the established tools do

They keep the same things apart

TestRail, Xray, Zephyr, Qase, Testmo; Linear, Jira, GitHub; Marker.io, BugHerd, Usersnap, Jam, Sentry; Canny, Productboard (research.md beside this page).

What they agree on

  • What someone sent (with its evidence) is apart from the work it becomes; triage decides which.
  • A test case is written once; each run of it records who, where and on which build; results are never edited, a retest adds one, and the latest per environment is where it stands.
  • Failing is one action, with as many defects as found, new or already filed.
  • A fix flows forward by itself; confirming it stays a person's step.
  • Closing needs a reason; reopening is a first-class action.
  • "Needs info" waits and flips back on a reply; internal comments stay apart from the reporter's view.
  • Ideas have their own intake; they're linked to roadmap items, never mixed with defects.

What none of them has

  • A developer that is an AI: an inbox of what changed, questions answered where the owner works, its own limited key, and rules the system enforces rather than trusts it to remember.
  • Checks written as walkthroughs the owner follows on the real page, with a card, in rounds.
  • That's the part worth building; issues can live here or in a company's tracker (section 8).

4 · Whose turn, and the words

One colour rule, a short set of words

The audit counted over 25 state words that collided. Now every pill says whose turn it is, and one sentence beside it says what. Red is kept for one meaning only: a failed try.

Yours

You act: a check to try, a question or a design to decide, a note to approve, an idea to place.

Claude's

I act: an issue to fix, a check to rewrite, a question you answered.

Big C's

A player acts: does their fix work now? (three days, then it's yours).

Closed

Nobody: with its reason (works now, won't do, same as, can't reproduce, withdrawn, retired).

ThingThe words (only these)
NoteWaiting for you (a player's) · Filed (as an issue, an idea, or answered) · Declined (with why)
IssueClaude's: to fix · Yours: Claude asks · Claude's: fixing · Yours (or a player's): ready to try · Closed: works now, won't do, same as, can't reproduce, withdrawn
CheckTo try · Passed · Failed · Can't try: later, blocked by an issue, check is wrong · Retired; shown per device when they differ
TryPassed · Failed · Can't try · and "replaced by" when corrected
IdeaNew · On the Roadmap (which item) · Shipped · Declined
AreaYours: round N to try · Claude's: fixing · Yours: Claude asks · Done

"Note" is what anyone sends, everywhere (on every page, in Notes you sent, in Feedback); "issue" is only ever the work. The words live in one catalog, so they're the same on every page and can be translated.

5 · The state rules

Every change, written once

Each rule is an event and what follows. The rebuild tests every combination, so no screen can disagree with another (the audit found five places where v1 did).

RuleWhenThen
R1A player sends a noteIt waits for you. None of its words, pictures or page reach Claude until you approve it (as an issue, joined to one, as an idea, answered as a question, or declined).
R2You approve a note as "same as" an issueIt joins that issue; its sender hears the issue's outcome, never another reporter's words or title. Unjoin is always possible from the issue's history.
R3A try failsOne Failed try, naming each issue: new ones (Claude's: to fix), or ones "still happening" (reopened: came back). An issue marked "doesn't stop it passing" doesn't hold the check.
R4An issue's fix is merged and liveIssue: ready to try. Each check it held, once none of its blocking issues remain unfixed: To try again, for whoever failed it on that device (a player's to that player first).
R5A try passesThe check is Passed on that device. Each issue on it: closed (works now) if you reported it or it's yours to confirm; a player's waits for them ("Big C's turn") and becomes yours after three days.
R6A try is correctedA new try replaces it, with why, both kept in the history. An issue that try alone created, with no other note or fix, closes as withdrawn; any other is only unlinked.
R7Can't tryLater: stays yours. Blocked by an issue: waits for it, back when it's fixed. Check is wrong: to Claude, who writes a new version (the earlier tries keep theirs).
R8An issue is moved to another area, deferred, closed or mergedIts checks are re-worked out by R3 to R5; a check is never left Failed with no open issue holding it.
R9A fix is rolled backIts issues return to Claude's: fixing; checks waiting on them leave To try.
R10The Mac Mini's run fails a checkAdds to the open issue (or files one). An automated pass never confirms a fix: only a person does.
R11An area's round is openaskdnd refuses to let Claude start fixing it (only sort and acknowledge), unless you mark an issue "this stops my round". One area is Claude's at a time: the lock has an owner and lapses if unused.
R12An area's issues are all closed and its checks passed or retiredIt's done. Issues found in its round that don't block it go to its next round or another area, so an area always finishes.
R13Claude asksThe issue, check or area shows Claude asks (yours); your answer moves it back to Claude's. A question is answered in one place, and an answer names the question it answers.
R14Anyone marks something from a page that's out of dateRefused with what changed (another tab, another person), never applied over it. Every page shows the change the moment it's made, everywhere it's open.
6 · Trust and privacy

Designed in, tested from day one

The audit found your standing rules broken in today's code. They're not patched now (the rebuild replaces that code); they're requirements, with their tests already written (kept on the branch privacy-rules).

T1Nothing unapproved reaches Claude. A player's note, its additions and their "still broken" words reach Claude only once you approve them: not the inbox, the API, a check's history or an area's list.
T2First names only. Claude never gets an email or a full name; who was viewing as is a first name too.
T3Words are labelled and quoted. Everything Claude reads says who wrote it and their role; notes and pages are quoted evidence, never instructions. A change prompted by anyone but you waits for you to merge it.
T4The page as drawn only when needed. Claude fetches a note's page only when the picture isn't enough; it can hold what a player sees and their DM doesn't.
T5Evidence keeps its visibility. A player's picture is kept from their DM, and so is anything written from it (an issue's title, a check); a reporter sees only their own note, its title and replies meant for them.
T6A key per actor and place. Claude's key on askdnd.ai, staging's and the Mac Mini's are different and limited: staging may only relay notes (which always wait for you); no key of Claude's can confirm a fix.
T7History is kept, private words can go. Events are never rewritten; a person's words can be erased on request without breaking the record.
T8Claude's inbox per listener. Each listener (the main chat, later an area's own) has its own read mark, so none takes another's items; nothing is skipped when two changes save out of order.
7 · The workflows

Every lifecycle, end to end

You · a player · Claude · askdnd. The ones the audit found missing are marked new.

Sending a note

1

Anyone: the feedback button. Nothing picked for them: what it is, words, the page's picture or one they add, marked up (Box, Arrow, Draw, Text; Undo apart). Hide while I take a screenshot steps aside (⇧⌘3 / ⇧⌘4 with ⌃ on a Mac, the side and volume buttons on an iPhone).

2

The line under Send says who reads it and when: "Claude reads it within a minute", or "No Claude session is listening; it waits" new.

3

You approve a player's: as an issue, same as one already filed (with a preview of what that does), as an idea, answered as a question, or declined with why. Undo for a minute new.

Claude fixes an area

1

Claude takes the first area in your order when its round is closed (R11), reads all of it, and asks what only you can decide (questions, a design as one decision).

2

Fixes it as one change; writes a check per issue (or adds the issue to one), each tried automatically before it reaches you new: its page opens and everything its steps name exists. "Fixed, no check needed" for a typo new.

3

askdnd: merged and live, its issues are ready to try; checks come back (R4); a rollback undoes it (R9). Passed checks the change touched are offered to re-try new.

You try a round

1

Start the round (an area, or everything yours). The card: steps, ✓ Passed (moves straight on; a corner card offers Undo and + Note), ✗ Something's wrong…, Can't try…, End round. Keys: P, F, C new.

2

Something's wrong…: the check's own issues first ("still happening?" reopens one) new, or a new one; does it stop the check passing? new; does it stop your round? new. Each is its own press; the first fails the try. The card folds after a fail.

3

Can't try…: later, blocked by an issue, or check is wrong (R7) new. Pressed the wrong one: Change result (R6).

4

End round any time; it resumes from For you new. Each try records your device and the deployed version.

Closing the loop

1

A player: does it work now? Yes, or still broken (words to you first). Asked for more by Claude, they answer on Notes you sent new.

2

Claude closes with a reason the sender is told: works now, won't do (as designed, with why), can't reproduce, same as new.

3

Reopen any closed issue; it says it came back new.

Areas

1

Move an issue to another area; rename, merge or archive an area; an empty area drops off new.

2

Each belongs to a Roadmap item; round 2, 3… in the same area; Done by R12.

Decisions

1

Claude asks one decision per mockup: For you shows one row; the mockup opens from the repo's copy (Manage › Documents) with its verdict and choices answered in askdnd new, recorded with the version you saw.

2

"Redraw" sends it back with your words; the next version is a new decision linked to the last.

8 · Built to lift out

Ready for another project, or a company

Its own pieces

Its own tables and pages, a workspace on every row, and one adapter the host fills in: who people are and their roles, viewing as one, where pictures are stored, and the policy hooks a company supplies (who can see, redact, notify).

Three capabilities, not five roles

Report, test, develop (a person or Claude), plus approve; a company maps its groups onto them later.

Events underneath

Every state is worked out from the record of events, and tested that way; nothing is rewritten.

Is the fix live?

The host's deploy tells askdnd which version is live (today: a script on your Mac); rollbacks included.

Issues here, or in their tracker

An issue lives here or in a company's Jira or Linear, with a link and an export, not two-way sync (the audit: the costliest, least distinctive part).

What stays askdnd's

The table's rules (a player's note waits for you; the DM never sees a player's character notes; first names only to Claude) are host policy, plugged into the hooks.

9 · Cut and deferred

What it doesn't do, on purpose

Cut

  • Two-way sync with other trackers (a link and an export instead).
  • Editing a try's result (a correcting try instead).
  • Five roles (three capabilities instead).

Deferred

  • An area in a chat of its own (note 123): after per-listener inboxes (T8), area locks with an owner (R11) and scoped keys (T6), then one trial area.
  • Severity, priority and assignment beyond one tester: designed for, not drawn.
10 · How it's built

In slices, each shippable

A clean rebuild (your word: migration isn't a concern). Today's notes and checks are converted once or let go.

  1. The record and the things: events, notes, issues, checks with versions, tries, rounds, questions, decisions, areas; the state rules R1 to R14 tested by trying every combination; T1, T2, T7 with the parked tests.
  2. Testing: For you (everything yours), the check, the card and its windows, Where it stands.
  3. Feedback: sending a note, approving, the issue page, Notes you sent.
  4. Claude's side: the inbox per listener, labelled and quoted reading, scoped keys, checks tried before they reach you.
  5. Decisions in askdnd, then the Roadmap on top (its own mockup).

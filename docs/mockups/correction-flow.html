Corrections · decided and built, Oct 30

Five steps, the first answer wins

When askdnd needs to know something about an item, such as what it costs, it starts at the top and stops at the first step that has an answer. Your own campaign's correction beats everything, so homebrew never has to change askdnd for anyone else.

Where you'll find it

  1. To make a correction: open any item from a character page, so its side panel shows. The Admin box at the bottom of that panel becomes a Correct this box, with a choice of who it's for.
  2. Your campaign's corrections are listed on Manage › House rules, beside your spell rulings. The DM can remove them there.
  3. askdnd's corrections stay on ⚙ Admin › Tool corrections, the page in your screenshot.
  4. To see why: the side panel says where the answer came from, under the item's cost.

Try it

Pick an item, then add a correction at either level. Example items, nothing here changes anything.

    What it means for a homebrew item

    Your DM makes up a sword that costs a Bonus Action to activate, in a campaign where D&D Beyond's description says nothing.

    1. Without a correction it lands on step 5: under Action, no cost shown.
    2. The DM opens it, picks Bonus action, This campaign. It moves to the Bonus action column for that table.
    3. No other campaign changes. If the same name appears elsewhere, it still follows steps 2 to 5 there.

    Questions for you

    1 · Where do campaign corrections live?

    A, recommended: on House rules. It's already the DM's page for "this table does it differently", so it stays one place.

    B: a new Corrections page in the campaign's Manage. Matches Tool corrections, but it's one more page, and spells and items get split apart.

    2 · Who can make them?

    A, recommended: the DM and admins for a campaign; only admins for everyone. The DM makes every ruling at their own table.

    B: admins only. Simpler, but your DM would have to ask you each time. Today you're both, so this matters when another group joins.

    3 · What can a campaign correct?

    A, recommended: all of it. Everything the Admin box can do today: what it costs, whether it's on Battle, hidden, inventory group.

    B: only what it costs. Smaller, but a homebrew item that should be hidden or regrouped would still need an askdnd-wide correction, which is what you're avoiding.

    4 · Should the panel say where the answer came from?

    A, recommended: yes, one quiet line. For example "From this campaign's house rule", so nobody wonders why two tables differ.

    B: only for the DM and admins. A cleaner panel for players, who can't change it anyway.

    5 · Items that need no action

    Lifewell Tattoo just happens when triggered. So do the Dragon-Touched Focus's Chromatic and Gem options.

    A, recommended: a small "No action needed" section under the columns, like On a hit, and a matching choice in Correct this.

    B: leave them under Action. No new section; a little misleading.

    6 · The Dragon-Touched Focus choice

    Metallic, Chromatic or Gem is different: it's one character's copy, not a whole campaign's. It fits step 4, the player picking from the options the item's own text gives, and steps 1 and 2 can still overrule it.

    A, recommended: build it after this, keeping the pick on the character, and move it into your own inventory list when that exists. Trah's note can go.

    B: wait for the inventory list. One place from the start, but the note stays for now.

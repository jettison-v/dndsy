Clearing filters

Every list in askdnd filters the same way, but clearing doesn't. On the Spell Book, Sheet's lists, the Item browser and Party, Clear appears only in a Showing only: … Clear all row above the list, once something is filtered, away from the chips you pressed. World and Notes you sent have Clear at the top of the filters instead, always showing. Battle's See all panel has no Clear at all. Below is Cole's Spell Book, the list you pointed at, drawn three ways. Press a few chips on each to see where Clear shows.

Where your eye already is

Clear sits at the right of the Filters heading, on every list, and shows only once something is filtered. No row above the list: the blue chips already say what's on, so the list keeps that room (your comment, Nov 16). On a phone, where the filters fold away, the folded button says how many are on and carries Clear.

B · One row above the list

"Showing only … Clear all", everywhere

Today's row above the list becomes the one place to clear, added to World, Notes you sent and Battle's panels; the Clear at the top of World's and Notes' filters goes. Closest to the design system as written, but it's where you already missed it.

C · Both

Clear at the top, and Clear all above the list

Both, on every list: hard to miss either way, at the cost of two buttons doing one thing.

One choice

Where should Clear go, on every filtered list?
  • A, at the top of the filters (my pick): Clear by the Filters heading, only once something's filtered; no row above the list, the blue chips say what's on.
  • B, one row above the list: "Showing only … Clear all" on every list, the column's Clear gone.
  • C, both: two Clears on every list.

Whichever you pick goes onto every list at once: the Spell Book, Sheet's Actions, Spells, Wild Shape, Inventory and Features, the Item browser, Change spells, Party, World, Notes you sent, and Battle's See all and + Add panels.

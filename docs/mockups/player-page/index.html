Trophy Case

A page per player that keeps what the recaps gave them and what their dice did: every award from the published sessions (Claude's, which you pick, and the dice's), and their own numbers from Table rolls and the sessions, across the whole campaign.

Where it lives: today a character has three pages, Battle · Sheet · Plan. Everything here is made up: Cole and the party, invented numbers. It's for fun, never a ranking of how anyone plays. Who talked stays on each session's page, so this page never compares people by talking.

Why here
  • Awards go to characters, so they sit with the character, one click from Battle.
  • Anyone can look at anyone's, from Party, like the Sheet.
  • A test campaign's twins get their own, apart from the real ones.
Where the numbers come from
  • Rolls everyone saw in Table rolls: askdnd's own and D&D Beyond's game log. A DM-only or private roll never counts.
  • Damage and healing: the damage and heal rolls.
  • Key moments: the published ones that link the character.
  • Awards: the published version of each recap.
Worth knowing
  • Rolls made on paper or before Table rolls existed aren't counted, so early sessions look quiet.
  • "Average d20" is facts only: no "lucky" or "unlucky" verdict.

What I'd build first

A, with the shelf, the tiles and the awards list from data askdnd already has. The d20 chart and the per-session sparkline come second. B can come later as links to each character's page, without the comparison columns.

The awards list includes Claude's (only the nominee the DM picked, once published) and the dice's. A trophy for something new (most healing in a night, say) is added in one place and shows on every shelf.

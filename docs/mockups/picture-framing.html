Framing a picture once, so every place shows the right part

Your note: "Let the user choose where the photo is displayed, like a profile photo. And see if the photos will have the same aspect ratio across the product." Today askdnd guesses: the small square is cut by what the picture is of (faces high for a person), and the wide band on the entry page is cut from the middle by the browser, which is how Kieran's band can lose the top of his head.

Where it lives

The Picture window, after Upload or picking a drawn one

Every picture already goes through the one Picture window (World entries, familiars and other creatures). Framing becomes its last step, and Frame it on a picture that's already there opens just that step. Whoever can change the picture can frame it (the DM for World entries, a player for their own creatures).

One switch for the table, one status for you

Today a player meets three things: the Live pill, a Game Mode switch kept in each browser, and a bridge pill that's really an admin's health check. This puts them in two places. Game Mode means "we're playing" and is on for the whole table at once. The Live pill says how your own sheet and askdnd are kept in step right now. The bridge is never a switch: it simply works whenever it can, in a game or between games.

Today
  • Game Mode, per browser: reads everyone's sheet every 2–3 minutes, shares your rolls, keeps the bridge awake 6 hours. Forget to turn it on and your rolls stay private without a word.
  • Live pill: when the copy was last read. Green only when your browser has Game Mode on.
  • Bridge pill (bottom right): askdnd's health as a whole ("connected, not checked yet"); clicking it runs a test on Vi.
Proposed
  • Game Mode for the table: anyone turns it on, every page in the campaign follows within a second, it ends after 6 hours or when someone ends it.
  • Live pill about you: "in step" when the bridge is working for your character, "updated 2 min ago" when askdnd is reading it on a timer, amber when something needs you.
  • Bridge health moves to Manage › askdnd › D&D Beyond bridge, for admins.

In a game and between games

Between games, nothing waits for Game Mode.The bridge keeps the rules it has today: a save on your D&D Beyond sheet is always read within seconds, and a change you make in askdnd is made on your sheet, whether or not a game is on. Using askdnd or a sheet keeps it awake for 30 minutes, so a player buying gear, levelling up or tidying their sheet on a Tuesday sees it in askdnd straight away, and a short rest taken in askdnd lands on D&D Beyond. Game Mode only adds what a game needs on top.

What moves

  • The bottom-right bridge pill goes to Manage › askdnd › D&D Beyond bridge, with its check, for admins only.
  • Game Mode's switch becomes Start / End for the table, in the same window.
  • Game Mode is stored for the campaign, not in each browser; pages learn it through the live connection.

What stays

  • The name Game Mode, and the Live pill where it is.
  • Refresh now, and Refresh everyone for reviewers.
  • The 6-hour game and its Keep going warning.
  • Everything the bridge does between games.

Decided (Oct 28)

  • Anyone at the table starts and ends it; ending asks first.
  • It ends after 6 hours or when someone ends it, not when the DM closes askdnd.
  • The DM's pill shows the party's slowest sheet.
  • Changes from askdnd only while your sheet can be reached now; built with the fight screen.

In short

Wren is a Moon Druid who fights up close in beast form and, as a person, casts from range. Wild Shape is a bonus action, and in beast form Wren keeps Concentration on a spell like Moonbeam. Two levels of Monk give Unarmored Defense (AC 16 out of armor) and a bonus-action unarmed strike.

Easy to forget

  • War Caster: when a creature leaves your reach, you can cast a spell at it instead of making an opportunity attack.
  • Luckstone (attuned): +1 to ability checks and saving throws, already included in the numbers.
  • Ring of Invisibility (attuned): turn invisible as a Magic action.
  • Wild Resurgence: once per turn, swap a spell slot for a Wild Shape use.

# The new-player tour and Help › What askdnd adds (built before the play state)

Brett chose A + C on 2026-10-24 (a tour started from the **?** menu, plus a Help tab to read) and gave the stop list
on 2026-10-26. It's built before the play state work because it's simpler and helps the table now. Mockup:
`docs/mockups/tour.html` (as Cole's player).

## How it behaves

- **Started by the person**, from **? › Take the tour** (phones: More › Help). Nothing pops up on its own.
- **On the real pages, with your own character.** Each stop dims the page, lights one thing, and shows a card:
  what it is and why it matters (no badges: Brett, 2026-10-26). Back, Next, the arrow
  keys and Esc. A group with several stops (Battle, Plan…) shows its parts as small bars on the card.
- **Nothing the tour shows is saved.** Pins and notes appear as examples on the page; Wild Shape is a preview that
  askdnd works out without saving it or telling the table; everything is put back when the stop changes or the tour
  ends. Your real pins, notes and form are never touched.
- **Conditional stops** show only when they apply: Spell Book if you cast spells, Wild Shape if you're a druid
  (the card says why it's shown). Without a character, the tour covers the campaign pages and Help.
- **Help › What askdnd adds**: the same stops to read, grouped (Before you play · On your turn · Rolling · Your
  character), each with **Show me**, which starts the tour at that group.

## The stops (Brett, 2026-10-26)

1. Previously on…
2. The campaign's memory
3. Battle: your turn on one screen; **+ Add** opens the side panel; the **pin** puts a row straight onto the
   column; open a row for **Pin** and **Your note**; the example on Battle with the note under it
4. Spell Book (casters): the filters; the damage and healing columns at each slot level
5. Wild Shape (druids): pick a form (numbers recalculated, actions switch); the form's full page opens large;
   **Normal** brings you back
6. Rolling
7. Game Mode and Live
8. Your character: Sheet, your whole character in one view; Reminders, so nothing is forgotten mid-battle
9. Plan: Battle combos, Leveling strategy, Personal quests, General notes, Shortlists (nothing is lost when you
   unprepare)
10. Party: everyone's numbers, and spells and items pooled by what they do (spreading potions, covering healing)
11. Help, any time

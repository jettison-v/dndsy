# Roadmap: the larger pieces, in order (2026-10-21)

askdnd has moved past small one-off iterations. This is the order for the larger pieces of work, and why. Each numbered
step ships on its own, keeps `main` working, and Brett reviews it on beta before the next one starts.

## Order

1. **Lists step 1: words and one note per thing.** Done (commit dd5384d): Battle, pins, bookmarks, Reminders, one note.
2. **Lists step 2: the Spell Book on the list pattern.** Done. Prepared spells only, chip filters with live counts, search
   that overrides filters, sort by level then name, the "Averages at" lens, compact rows with the note line, and the
   side panel sliding in from the window edge. Its pieces are built as **shared components** (filter column, chip,
   active-filter row, list row, view switch, side panel) so the next lists reuse them instead of copying markup.
   - Small item riding along: the character name in the menu bar opens your character in one click; the ▾ next to
     it switches characters (Brett, 2026-10-21).
3. **Lists step 3: Sheet › Spells** with the Ready · Shortlist · All views on the same components. Done, with a
   prepared icon (✓ / grey ○, plus an Always tag) on each row and a Prepared / Not prepared filter.
4. **Battle: fewer automatic choices** (Brett, 2026-10-21). Today each column fills itself with "common" options. To
   encourage planning and learning the tool, Battle would start with only what the player pinned (plus what everyone
   can do), and each column says "Pin options from See all (N)". Done: the columns show only pins to anyone who can
   see them; the DM (who can't) still sees the usual options.
5. **Live updates without a refresh** (Brett, 2026-10-21). A change made in a side panel or pop-up should show on the
   page behind it straight away. Known cases:
   - Pin in a side panel: the Battle column and the spell bar's pinned chips.
   - Pin or bookmark in a side panel: the row's icons on Sheet and in the Spell Book, and which view it's in
     (Shortlist), including the Shortlist that pinning adds.
   - Remind me: the Reminders box on Battle and Plan.
   - Your note: the note line under the row (Battle, Sheet, Spell Book, Plan, Reminders).
   - Unpin or un-remind from a row or the Reminders box: the same places.
   How: each mark or note change announces itself (an HTMX event with what changed); the parts of the page that show
   it refresh themselves (Battle's body the way Game Mode already does; list rows in place).
   **5a done:** pin, bookmark and remind announce themselves (`marksChanged`); Battle reloads its body and rows update in
   place. **5b done:** a saved note announces itself (`noteChanged`): note lines change in place, Battle reloads its body,
   and Plan's Reminders and Shortlist tabs refresh (keeping the open tab and the counts).
6. **Lists step 4: Plan.** The Shortlist and Reminders tabs on the shared list, free-text reminders.
   Also on Plan (Brett, 2026-10-21):
   - Saving a new or edited item keeps you where you were: no full-page reload, no jump back to the first tab.
   - Combos, Leveling, Quests and Notes become compact rows like the other lists, with the full details in the side
     panel.
   - Combos, Leveling, Quests and Notes can be reordered by drag and drop.
   - Notes stay as tiles, three per row, with a capped height so more fit on screen (the full note in the side panel).
7. **Magic items on Battle** (Brett, 2026-10-21; example: Cole's Ring of Invisibility, which askdnd shows today only as
   passive gear).
   - **Item actions:** an item that grants an action shows in the right Battle column with its cost, pinnable like
     anything else (the ring: a Magic action to turn Invisible, a Bonus Action to become visible). Read from the item
     text where it's clear; an audited list in `app/data` (like the roll recipes) for the rest.
   - **Condition tracker:** a pill under the Battle header, like Concentration: what you are now (Invisible first), what
     it does under the 2024 rules (from `rules_terms`), and what ends it; for the ring, attacking or casting doesn't end
     it, unlike the Invisibility spell. Everyone at the table sees it.
   - **Roll window:** while Invisible, your attack rolls offer Advantage with the reason ("Invisible: Ring of
     Invisibility"); a creature that can see you cancels it, and the DM decides.
   - **Attunement:** slots used out of 3 on Inventory and in the side panel.
8. **Lists step 5: Wild Shape** forms as rows with pictures.
9. **Design system checkpoint.** By now the components have several users. Document them in DESIGN.md › Components
   (with the glossary and the list pattern), and add a living page that shows each component with real markup, so
   future screens and mockups start from it. Includes one pass over the whole app to use the text-size scale (Brett, 2026-10-21:
   "consistent on text size throughout the whole app") and on control sizes (the search box is too large).
10. **Data organization** (docs/plans/2026-10-21-data-organization.md, four steps): characters separate from campaigns,
   roles per campaign, tool corrections and house rules, more than one campaign. Brett's 8 decisions are needed first;
   they can be answered while steps 2–8 are built.
11. **More lists on the pattern:** Inventory and Features (any time after step 9), then Campaign (people, places,
   factions, items) after step 10, because the campaign switcher changes what the Campaign lists show.

## Why this order

- **Lists before data organization.** The list work only touches character pages and the player's own marks, which the
  data plan keeps on the character. Doing the data plan first would delay visible improvements without saving rework.
- **Components while building, documented after.** The first list (Spell Book) defines the components; the second
  (Sheet spells) proves they're reusable. Writing the design system before either exists would mean guessing, then
  rewriting it.
- **Campaign lists last.** They depend on how campaigns are separated (step 10).

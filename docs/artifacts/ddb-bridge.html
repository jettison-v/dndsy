The D&D Beyond bridge

A small Chrome extension in each player's own D&D Beyond tab. It hears every change the sheet saves, and makes askdnd's changes there, as that player. When a player's sheet is closed, the DM's browser can make them instead.

The two never quietly disagree. askdnd counts a change only once it reads it back from D&D Beyond. One it can't make says Didn't go through, with Try again and Undo, and nothing on the sheet is written over.

What it changes on D&D Beyond

LifeHit points and temporary hit points, Death Saving Throws, Heroic Inspiration, conditions and Exhaustion, Hit Dice (D&D Beyond takes those only as a Short Rest, so askdnd spends them that way)
MagicSpell slots, Pact Magic slots, class features' limited uses (one or several from a pool, like Lay On Hands), prepared spells (a spell's Prepare, or Prepare spells for several at once)
InventoryHow many, equipped, attuned, charges, coins, adding another of an item, removing one, moving it into or out of a container
GivingGive hands an item to another character at the table: it leaves one sheet and a fresh one is added to the other, since D&D Beyond can't move an item between characters (charges used and attunement stay behind). The DM's Give loot adds an item from D&D Beyond's 2024 books, or coins. Both sheets must be reachable.
RollsA roll made on the sheet comes into Table rolls marked "on D&D Beyond". A player's askdnd roll goes into D&D Beyond's Game Log from their open sheet, marked as askdnd's (ⓐ after its name), never as D&D Beyond's (extension 0.9.3).
The fightOn D&D Beyond's VTT it listens: the order, the round and whose turn, for the band above the name on askdnd's character pages and Party. The one thing it writes there is initiative numbers, with Initiative from askdnd (a Labs feature, through the DM's open VTT).
CreaturesWith Your creatures: a familiar's or companion's Hit Points on the sheet's Extras, the familiar that's here switched on with its creature type and name (the others switched off, never deleted), and a new familiar form added.
Left outExperience points, custom names, custom and homebrew items, and a container with things in it: those are handed over or changed on D&D Beyond.

The DM as a backup

When a player's sheet is closedThe DM's Chrome, with any D&D Beyond page open and the extension, makes that character's changes, using the DM's own rights on D&D Beyond (a D&D Beyond campaign's DM can change its players' sheets). The player's own open sheet always goes first.
Only at this tableOnly characters in the campaign's own D&D Beyond game, which askdnd is told on Manage › Settings; never a character from anywhere else.
Who made itD&D Beyond doesn't say who changed a sheet, so askdnd's log does: "(made through the DM)", and the DM's Live window lists the newest.
The Live pillGreen: your own sheet is connected. Yellow: through the DM (it still asks you to open your sheet, since only your sheet sends rolls to D&D Beyond's Game Log). Red: can't be reached while the table is playing. Grey: no one is connected.

When it's on

An askdnd page opens, or someone uses oneOn for 30 more minutes. A character's page also reads D&D Beyond first if askdnd's copy is about 2 minutes old or more.
A D&D Beyond sheet opens, or someone changes itOn for 30 more minutes. A change saved on D&D Beyond is always recorded, even if the bridge was asleep.
A fight on D&D Beyond's VTT, or a change on its wayOn for as long as either lasts, however quiet.
No one around for 30 minutesAsleep: it stops checking D&D Beyond in the background. The next page or click wakes it.

When something goes wrong

  • Tries againD&D Beyond says OK but drops a change, or a sheet tab doesn't answer: sent again, up to 3 times.
  • Works it out againSomeone changed the sheet a moment before: the change is worked out again from the new numbers.
  • Didn't go throughA number askdnd can't explain, or 3 failed tries: nothing is written over; its corner card and the Live window offer Try again and Undo.
  • Can't reach itNo sheet open and no DM to carry it, or a phone: a change's window says so the moment it opens and offers no button that would be refused (askdnd's own parts, like its creatures, still work). One already on its way when the sheet closes says Made once the sheet is open, and is made by itself when the sheet opens again (ticked off if someone made it on D&D Beyond meanwhile).
  • Changed on D&D BeyondChanges made on D&D Beyond itself show in askdnd within seconds, and are logged whichever read sees them first.
  • Shown in placeEvery open copy of the sheet shows askdnd's change without reloading, so it can't save an old number back.

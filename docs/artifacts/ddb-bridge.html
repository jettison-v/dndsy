The D&D Beyond bridge

askdnd does the playing; D&D Beyond stays correct. A small Chrome extension in your own signed-in D&D Beyond tab hears every change the sheet saves and makes askdnd's changes there, as you. It works like middleware: it keeps what you did, sends the end state, checks every result, and repairs itself when something goes wrong.

D&D Beyond confirms every change.askdnd counts a change only after reading it back from D&D Beyond. Until then it's waiting; if it can't be made, askdnd lists the end state to make by hand. The two never quietly disagree.

How a change travels

Five steps, and three safety nets

askdnd

1
You act. "Drank a Potion of Healing, rolled 4." askdnd keeps that intent in its outbox for the character, in the database.
2
One end state. Everything waiting for the character becomes one batch of final totals, worked out from a fresh read of D&D Beyond.
5
Read back. askdnd reads D&D Beyond itself, field by field. Only a match counts.

The extension, in your tab

3
Checks first. It reads the sheet as you. A total already there is skipped (never a double drink); a total still at its old value is written; anything else means someone changed it, and nothing is written.
4
Writes, one at a time. The sheet's own two requests (hit points, an item's count), then every open copy of the sheet reloads so none saves an old number back.

D&D Beyond

·
The record. Nothing is stored about you: the extension uses the 5-minute token D&D Beyond gives your own tab. It may only change the sheet that's open, only those two things.
·

Pulled every 30 secondsEach open sheet is read again; a missed save, or a "done" that never arrived, is settled here.

Only while you play

Awake for the game, asleep the rest of the week

The bridge never runs in the background between games. Asleep, askdnd and the extension read nothing from D&D Beyond: no pulls, no checks, no deliveries.

Wakes when awake

  • Someone turns on Game Mode (and on every page they open while it's on).
  • A change is made through askdnd: a potion drunk, hit points taken or healed.
  • Someone runs the check from the corner badge.

Sleeps when asleep

  • 30 minutes pass with nothing happening (never while a change is still waiting).
  • Game Mode is turned off, once any waiting change is through.
  • 6 hours at most, whatever happens. Anything still waiting is then listed to make by hand, never left hanging.

Where it stands

A proof that works, not yet a feature

Built, on your computer only

  • The extension (load unpacked) and askdnd's side, switched on by DDB_BRIDGE=1; never on beta.
  • Drink a potion, take or heal hit points; the end-state list for changes made by hand.
  • The status badge on every page, the read check, the pre-game round trip on Vi.
  • Sleeps unless the table is playing or testing: 30 minutes idle, Game Mode off, 6 hours at most.
  • Lessons from Foundry's DDB Importer (read, not copied): one write at a time, end states win, trust only the read-back.

Before it's real

  • More changes: spell slots, class resources, conditions, coins, attunement (D&D Beyond has requests for each).
  • Players install the extension (a private Chrome Web Store listing, or load unpacked).
  • Unofficial: D&D Beyond can change its requests or enforce its terms. The read-back and the checks turn that into "make by hand", never wrong numbers. Brett accepts the risk and tells the table first.
  • Merge it into the roadmap as Phase 1's foundation (play state, fight screen).

The D&D Beyond bridge

A small Chrome extension in each player's own D&D Beyond tab. It hears every change the sheet saves, and makes askdnd's changes there, as that player.

The two never quietly disagree. askdnd counts a change only once it reads it back from D&D Beyond. Anything it can't make is listed to make by hand.

What it changes on D&D Beyond

LifeHit points and temporary hit points, Death Saving Throws, Heroic Inspiration, conditions and Exhaustion, Hit Dice (D&D Beyond takes those only as a Short Rest, so askdnd spends them that way)
MagicSpell slots, Pact Magic slots, class features' limited uses
InventoryHow many, equipped, attuned, charges, coins, adding another of an item, removing one, moving it into or out of a container
RollsA roll made on the sheet comes into Table rolls marked "on D&D Beyond". A player's askdnd roll goes into D&D Beyond's Game Log from their open sheet, marked as askdnd's (ⓐ after its name), never as D&D Beyond's (extension 0.9.3).
The fightOn D&D Beyond's VTT it only listens: the order, the round and whose turn, for the band above the name on askdnd's character pages and Party (extension 0.9.4). It never changes the VTT.
Left outExperience points, custom names and custom items. Prepared spells come with casting in Phase 2.

When it's on

An askdnd page opens, or someone uses oneOn for 30 more minutes. A character's page also reads D&D Beyond first if askdnd's copy is more than 2 minutes old.
A D&D Beyond sheet opens, or someone changes itOn for 30 more minutes. A change saved on D&D Beyond is always recorded, even if the bridge was asleep.
Game Mode switched onOn for the whole game, 6 hours, however quiet. A message 10 minutes before the end offers Keep going.
No one around for 30 minutesAsleep: it stops checking D&D Beyond in the background. The next page or click wakes it.

When something goes wrong

  • Tries againD&D Beyond says OK but drops a change, or a sheet tab doesn't answer: sent again, up to 3 times.
  • Works it out againSomeone changed the sheet a moment before: the change is worked out again from the new numbers.
  • Make by handA number askdnd can't explain, or 3 failed tries: nothing is written over, and askdnd lists the end state to make.
  • WaitsNo sheet open, or a phone: the change waits and goes out when a sheet opens (6 hours at most).
  • Changed on D&D BeyondChanges made on D&D Beyond itself show in askdnd within seconds, and are logged whichever read sees them first.
  • Shown in placeEvery open copy of the sheet shows askdnd's change without reloading, so it can't save an old number back.
Every change tested on Vi in Brett's Chrome against the real D&D Beyond (Manage › askdnd › D&D Beyond bridge › Check everything runs them all again, out and back), plus the automated tests. On beta since Oct 28 for the test campaign; for the table once the Chrome Web Store listing is approved. Admins change the timers on Manage › askdnd › D&D Beyond bridge. D&D Beyond's terms don't allow tools like this: nothing is disguised, Brett accepts the risk and tells the table, and if D&D Beyond blocks it, askdnd falls back to make by hand.

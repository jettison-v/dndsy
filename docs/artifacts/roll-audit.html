Roll Audit

Every way a roll is made, step by step, as close-ups: the roll card, and once there's a result, the Table rolls entry it made beside it. Look for the right number and dice, the right choices at each step, and nothing missing or extra. Comment on a screen and your note comes to Claude.

Close-ups taken November 10, after the rebuild, at a 1440 × 900 laptop size on the local copy of the campaign (Cole, Trah, No Name, Big C). The Cast screens come from askdnd's test setup, where a stand-in D&D Beyond sheet is connected. Click a screen to see it full size; arrow keys step through.

Rebuilt since the first audit
  • One plan per roll, from askdnd itself. Every roll button now carries everything about its roll, worked out on the server from the sheet and the checked rules: what kind of roll it is, its bonus, a spell's dice at every level, what can be added and when, its notes. The card only plays it. Before, only Battle's attack buttons had this; every other button had the browser piece a roll together from the button, once even reading a save's DC out of the spell's words.
  • One window. The old roll window is gone. Death saves, Table rolls' follow-ups and Shift-click are on the card, and the card has no Labs switch.
  • One rule for where it opens, in the design system: beside its row, wholly on screen, never over its button, placed once and never moving between steps.
  • A test that plays every roll button on Battle, Sheet, the Spell Book and Table rolls' Roll, for Cole, Big C, No Name and Trah (about 350 buttons), checking that what's sent is what the plan says, that there are no errors, and where the card sits, at 1440 and 1280 wide and on a phone. A new roll button is checked the day it exists.
  • What that test found, now fixed: Spiritual Weapon's levels were offered as a step and sent as if they were Grapple; Hunter's Mark's and Absorb Elements' Roll had nothing to roll; a follow-up from Table rolls broke the card; the card drifted a few pixels between steps and slid back down after a shorter step.
  • Found making these close-ups, fixed: Missed didn't reach Table rolls until a reload, and a missed roll still offered Roll damage; several beams' damage showed each beam's total in huge red mid-line, and the thrown dice could show the first beam's number as the total; Eldritch Blast said "2 times" (now "2 beams", "Beam 1").
  • Where the card opens isn't shown here (close-ups leave out the page): the every-button test checks it for every roll.
  • Fixed in the first audit (still in these screens): Cast and roll in one press, riders only where they apply, nothing for a miss on a 20, a card from Roll staying put, two wording slips.
Not in these screens
  • A Wild Shape form's attacks, and a familiar's or a summon's rolls (their buttons carry plans the same way; the test doesn't play them yet)
  • Using an item, such as a potion (its own Use window)
  • Bless or Bane given by someone else
  • What the DM sees of a player's roll
  • Rolls sent into D&D Beyond's Game Log
  • Hunter's Mark's and Spiritual Weapon's fixes (locally they offer Cast; the test plays both)

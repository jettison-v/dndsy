Roll Audit

Every way a roll is made today, screen by screen, with the roll card's Labs switch on. Look for the right number and dice, the right choices at each step, and nothing missing or extra. Comment on a screen and your note comes to Claude.

Taken November 10 at a 1440 × 900 laptop size on the local copy of the campaign (Cole, Trah, No Name, Big C). The Cast screens come from askdnd's test setup, where a stand-in D&D Beyond sheet is connected. Click a screen to see it full size; arrow keys step through.

Found and fixed while taking these
  • Cast and roll spent the slot, then showed a second screen whose Cast at changed nothing. Now one press casts and rolls at the level cast (section 9).
  • Roll without spending a slot in the Cast window did nothing. It opens the card now.
  • Riders that couldn't apply. Eldritch Blast listed Savage Attacker, Stunning Strike and Empowered Strikes (a weapon's or an Unarmed Strike's); the Maul listed Guided Strike and Favored by the Gods under On a hit. Each rider now shows only where its own words say it applies, at the time they say.
  • A natural 20 still offered Missed and after-a-miss options. A 20 always hits, so they're gone on a 20.
  • A card from the Roll window (Hit Point Die, Any dice) jumped to the top-left corner after rolling. It stays where it opened.
  • Healing Word's line said "+ spellcasting modifier" twice. Once now.
  • Two beams' totals broke onto two lines in Table rolls. One line now.
  • Old and new side by side: all 302 roll buttons on the party's pages (Battle, Sheet, the Roll window) send the same roll from the card and from the older window. The window was missing the riders; it has them now.
Not in these screens
  • A Wild Shape form's attacks
  • A familiar's or a summon's rolls (Pip, Burt)
  • Using an item, such as a potion
  • Bless or Bane given by someone else
  • A phone, where the card rises from the bottom
  • What the DM sees of a player's roll
  • Rolls sent into D&D Beyond's Game Log

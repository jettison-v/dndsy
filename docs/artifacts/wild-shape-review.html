Playing in Wild Shape

What happens in askdnd when Cole becomes a Polar Bear, on every screen, and the choices for making a form something you play, not only something you see.

Scanned Nov 3 on the local copy of beta: Cole (Druid 6, Circle of the Moon / Monk 2) in the Polar Bear form during a fight, as his player and as the DM, on a computer and a phone. 44 screens and 30 windows.

The rules askdnd follows

ShiftingA Bonus Action and one Wild Shape use. Leaving early is a Bonus Action too; the form also ends after half your Druid level in hours, on a new Wild Shape, Incapacitated or death.
Hit PointsYou keep your own Hit Points and Hit Point Dice. Shifting gives Temporary Hit Points: your Druid level, three times that with Circle of the Moon (Cole: 18). They don't add to ones you have (keep the higher), are lost first, and stay after you leave the form until used.
NumbersThe Beast's Str, Dex, Con, AC, Speed and attacks; your Int, Wis, Cha, proficiencies, feats and class features. Circle of the Moon's AC is at least 13 + Wisdom.
SpellsNo casting, except Circle of the Moon Spells (Cure Wounds, Moonbeam, Starry Wisp, Conjure Animals for Cole). Concentration isn't broken by shifting.
What you loseSpecies traits (Cole's Mask of the Wild, Fey Ancestry). Equipment falls, merges or is worn: the DM decides.
On D&D BeyondNo "in a form" state. It keeps the Wild Shape uses and Temporary Hit Points, which the bridge can change. The form itself stays askdnd's.

Already right

  • The form's numbers: AC 17 (Circle Forms), Speed, saves and skills with Cole's proficiencies, feats and class features carried over.
  • Battle's columns: the bear's Multiattack and Rend, Cole's own Unarmed Strike and Monk features as "Yours", the Circle of the Moon spells with Cast, Leave the form and Wild Shape again as Bonus Actions.
  • The Hit points window knows the form ("Cole 51 of 51 · Polar Bear") and its defenses.
  • Everyone at the table sees the switch when it's made.

What the scan found

Can't play it

Shifting does nothing on D&D Beyond

  • Picking a form changes the page only: no Wild Shape use is spent and no Temporary Hit Points are given; Battle says "+18 temp on shifting" but they never arrive.
  • The form switch at the top and "Wild Shape into another known form" in Bonus Action are two ways in, and neither says it costs a Bonus Action and a use.
Can't play it

Battle in a form

  • No − and + on Hit Points. The form's card shows 51 with no way to take damage or heal; today it's Party's Hit Points, or back to Normal.
  • The Status row is gone: Concentration, conditions, + Condition and Rest disappear while in a form.
  • On a phone the form's card comes first and Hit Points are inside it, below the fold (the usability round put Hit Points first).
No warning

Sheet in a form

  • Nothing says Cole is a bear. It shows his own AC 16, Speed 45 and saves, which is right for a character sheet, but with no band.
  • It offers what the bear can't do, with no word: Thorn Whip and his other druid spells to roll (only the Circle of the Moon spells work in a form), Mask of the Wild (a species trait, lost), Drink on a potion, the Ring of Invisibility and the soul coins (equipment: the DM decides).
Wrong number

Everywhere else

  • Party, the fight's rows and askdnd's table show AC 16 (Cole's own; the bear's is 17) and never say he's a bear. The fight band shows his portrait.
  • His short version (Party's card) shows his own AC, Speed and saves.
No warning

Cast in a form

  • Cast lets him cast Healing Word as a bear and spends the slot; it isn't a Circle of the Moon spell. The window says nothing.

Choices for you

1. Shifting

A · Shift is a changeRecommended

Picking a form spends a Wild Shape use and gives the Temporary Hit Points through the bridge (keep the higher, as the Hit points window does), with Undo, like Cast. Leave is its own button. The form itself stays askdnd's.

B · Show it, remind me

As today, plus a card: "Mark one Wild Shape use and 18 Temporary Hit Points on D&D Beyond".

2. Battle in a form

A · The form's numbers in the usual topRecommended

Battle's own top row, with the bear's AC, Speed and saves in place of Cole's and his Hit Points tile with − and +. The Status row stays (Concentration, conditions, Rest). A slim band names the form, with Leave. Phones keep Hit Points first.

B · Keep the green card, add what's missing

The form's card as today, with − and + added to its Hit Points and the Status row put back under it.

3. Sheet in a form

A · A band, and what a form can't do greyedRecommended

"In Wild Shape: Polar Bear. Battle shows the form." Sheet keeps Cole's own numbers, and greys what can't be used in a form, each with its reason (a spell that isn't a Circle spell, a species trait); equipment says "the DM decides". Nothing is hidden.

B · A band only

The band, nothing greyed.

4. Everyone else

A · Show the formRecommended

"Cole (Polar Bear)" with the bear's picture and AC on Party, the fight's rows, askdnd's table and the fight band; his rolls in Table rolls say "Cole (Polar Bear)".

B · A form badge only

Cole as today, with a small Wild Shape badge after his name.

5. Cast in a form

A · Say it, don't stop itRecommended

Cast for a spell the form can't cast says so ("Only Circle of the Moon Spells in a form") and still lets you go on: the DM rules, and askdnd doesn't enforce.

B · Not offered in a form

Only the spells the form can cast show Cast.

What I'd build first

  1. Battle you can play in: Hit Points with − and +, the Status row, phones with Hit Points first (choice 2).
  2. Shifting as a change: the use and the Temporary Hit Points through the bridge, Leave (choice 1).
  3. Everyone else sees the form, with the bear's AC (choice 4).
  4. Sheet's band and greyed actions, and Cast's word (choices 3 and 5).

Each step is tried on the local copy of beta first, then on Roscoe Norell (the Druid test character) against the real D&D Beyond, with a live test for shifting.

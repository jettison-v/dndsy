The UI and UX pass

Before the VTT, everything askdnd shows was opened and checked, the way the design system started on Oct 22, this time for how things work as well as how they look. First what the scan found, then what I'll fix without asking (each breaks a rule the design system already has), then seven choices that are yours. Answers save as you go. Tell me in chat when you're done.

Answers can't be saved in this view. You can read everything, and answer in chat instead.

What was scanned

Every page askdnd links to, as a player, the DM and an admin, on a phone (375 px) and a desktop (1280 px), with each tab clicked, and every kind of side panel, modal and small window opened from each page once. The small windows that change D&D Beyond (Cast, Use one, Rest, Inspiration, Hit points) were opened on Cathartic in VTT test, and the moment while a change is on its way was drawn on purpose. Alongside the screenshots, the scan recorded how each filter, notes switch, D&D Beyond mark and tile is built.

Fixed without asking

Each of these breaks something the design system already says, or is a plain bug. They're built in the rounds below; say so in the notes at the end if one shouldn't be.

Party › Items: an item's name one letter per line Bug

On a phone, an item many characters carry (Potion of Healing: six portraits, some ×2) squeezes its name column until it breaks after every letter. Fix: the portraits wrap under the name when they don't fit beside it.

The changing mark lands on the HP tile's − and + Bug

The ↻ "on its way to D&D Beyond" mark is pinned to every tile's top right corner, which is where − and + sit on Hit Points, so the two overlap while a change travels. With the buttons in the tile, "56 / 66" also breaks onto two lines. On Resources the ↻ sits on the last pip. Fix: question 1 (the mark) and question 4 (the tile) together.

The window's buttons sit in different places Rule

The design system puts a window's actions in its foot: the main one first, Cancel as a quiet link, Delete at the far end. Today Rest's "Take a Short Rest" is on the right, Hit points' sits alone under its working, Inspiration has Cancel and the others don't, and a battle combo's Delete is plain text. Fix: every window follows the rule, Cancel wherever a window makes a change.

The selected filter is red on History Rule

Chips are blue when on everywhere else (Wild Shape forms, Spells, the Reader). Manage › History and askdnd history use red. Fix: blue.

The Spell Book's ✕ on its own line on a phone Rule

Every other panel and modal has what it belongs to, its name and ✕ in one head. On a phone the Spell Book's ✕ drops below the title. Fix: the shared head, at every width.

Long page titles break onto two big lines on a phone Rule

"askdnd admin", "Backups and imports", "Tool corrections", "D&D Beyond bridge" and "Design system" take two lines at 30 px. Fix: page titles drop to 26 px on phones and balance their lines.

A page's own action floats in different places Rule

"+ New entry" sits at the right under Campaign's title, "+ Add session" is centred, "Refresh all from D&D Beyond" is on the left under Table's title, and "+ Add to the table" is a red button midway down. Fix: a page's own action sits at the right end of its title row, a secondary button; red stays for the one next step on a page (Review it, Draft the next one).

Resource names are red links in the Party card Rule

On a character's card from Party, Luck Points and Focus Points are underlined red links; on Battle and Party they're plain names that open the same window. Fix: plain names everywhere, opening the window.

The Adv badge hangs out of its tile on a phone Rule

Initiative's and the saves' Adv badges sit on the tile's bottom edge, half outside it. Fix: the tile's foot (question 4) holds badges.

Prepare spells: the top wraps on narrower screens Bug

The title, the count and ✕ break onto loose rows, and the filter chips wrap unevenly. Fix: on narrower screens the count goes under the title as one line with its boxes, ✕ stays beside the title, and the filters fold into one Filters line, as the Spell Book does.

"Last check: Not run since askdnd started" and similar in-between words Rule

A few admin cards say how something stands in words a person can't act on. Fix: each says what it means and what to do, or nothing.

How it gets built

One step at a time in this session, each tried on your computer first, then on beta.

The parts, in the design system firstThe tile, the D&D Beyond mark, the filter's start, Full notes, the window's foot, the panel's head and tab bars on phones, drawn on Manage › Design system so you see each once, in every state (a change on its way, a change given up, an empty list, a long name).
The character pages and their windowsBattle, Sheet, Plan, every small window and side panel, and Prepare spells, using those parts.
Everything elseStory, Campaign, Party, Help, and Manage for the DM and for askdnd; coins.
Guarded so it staysTests for the new rules (a tile with a change on its way, buttons inside it and a long number holds together; every list starts as decided; every list with notes has Full notes), the scan once more, docs/DESIGN.md and the Help pages brought to the end state. Then Phase 1 is complete and the VTT starts.

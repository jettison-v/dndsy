How the bridge keeps your sheet and askdnd in step

Two ways, always: a change you make on your D&D Beyond sheet reaches askdnd within seconds, and a change you make in askdnd is made on your sheet, as you, and counts only once askdnd reads it back. These four pictures show the parts, the path a change takes, and the timings everything runs on.

30 sThe extension checks in from each open sheet, while the bridge is awake
90 sA sheet heard this recently can be reached: askdnd takes a change for it
3 minA waiting change whose sheet went quiet this long waits for the sheet
3 triesA change D&D Beyond doesn't take is sent again, then says Didn't go through, with Try again and Undo
30 min · 6 hAwake after anyone uses askdnd or a sheet · during a fight

Your four buckets, with each campaign seeing only the books it should, and no checks while a game is running. Fight-screen stat blocks, the Spell Book's lists, Wild Shape forms and rules answers all build on this, so it's settled before they're built.

Your constraint

Checked once, not every time a game loads

  1. When the campaign is made, it gets its list of books. The default is the SRD plus the 2024 Player's Handbook, Dungeon Master's Guide and Monster Manual, which is what askdnd assumes today.
  2. The DM or an admin changes it on Manage › Settings › Books, any time: each book on or off.
  3. Every lookup filters by it in the one place lookups already go through. There are no extra checks on page loads and no calls to D&D Beyond.
Checked on Cole's sheet today

What D&D Beyond does, and what askdnd adds

D&D Beyond switches books on per character, by the player, in the character's own settings, with homebrew as a separate switch. Its campaign data names the DM and the characters but not which books are shared. Every spell and item on a sheet names its book.

Automatic

What a player has

  • Spells known and prepared, and inventory, come from D&D Beyond, already limited by the player's own settings there.
  • askdnd shows what's on the sheet. Nothing to build.
The books toggle

Beyond the sheet

  • Spells you could prepare: D&D Beyond also sends your class's whole list at your level, with each spell's text, from every book. That's All you could prepare and the Spell Book.
  • Items you don't carry, monsters, rules text: D&D Beyond sends none, so they come from the SRD and the campaign's books in askdnd (Wild Shape forms, stat blocks, loot, rules answers).
  • The books toggle filters both, instead of today's fixed three books.

A sheet with a book the campaign has off (say, a Xanathar's spell while Xanathar's is off) still shows it, because it's on the sheet, labelled with its book. askdnd doesn't remove anything; the DM rules on it.

Two lines we keep

What makes it safe to share

Public means licensed, not just free to read.Only the SRD comes with a license to store and show it. Anything that's free to read on D&D Beyond but isn't in the SRD is treated as private rules.
Enhancements are in our own words.Our summary of Moonbeam, informed by the DMG, is ours and goes to everyone. A paragraph copied from the DMG would really be the book, so enhancements summarize and never quote.
Your question

When askdnd runs inventory and spells

Once askdnd keeps play state (casting spends slots, inventory in play), things change during a session that D&D Beyond never sees. Each lands in a bucket, so there's no gap.

From the sheet

D&D Beyond's spells and items

  • Their text comes with the sheet, as today.
  • askdnd stores only the play numbers: slots spent, charges left, potions used, who holds it.
  • No book text is copied, and the books toggle doesn't apply: it's already the player's.
Put into play by askdnd

Loot the DM hands out, homebrew

  • Picked from the campaign's books, so the toggle applies at that moment.
  • It becomes campaign data: a copy with its book and entry, its details, and its numbers (quantity, charges, attuned).
  • Book text in it stays in that campaign's data, never in the code. Switching the book off later keeps the copy, labelled.
  • Given to another character: the copy, or the play numbers, move to them. D&D Beyond can't be written to, so askdnd reminds both players to update their sheets. When the item reaches a sheet later, the D&D Beyond merge links it to askdnd's copy instead of counting it twice, and lists it under "what changed".
  • Spells: spells known and prepared stay D&D Beyond's. askdnd spends slots and tracks concentration. The only spells that arrive through askdnd come on items (a scroll, a wand), as part of that item's copy.
  • An unidentified item (a potion the party hasn't tried): the DM hands out the real entry, and players see only what they've learned, as with monsters.
Back to D&D Beyond: a checklist, not write-back. D&D Beyond can't be written to officially. Other tabletops either copy the character and own play from then on (Foundry's DDB Importer, whose write-back is limited, paid, and needs each player's D&D Beyond login cookie) or leave inventory on D&D Beyond (Owlbear Rodeo and Roll20 with Beyond20 only pass rolls along). askdnd copies and owns play, and keeps a Copy to D&D Beyond checklist per character: "Add Flame Tongue · 2 fewer Potions of Healing · +150 gp", for the player to make on D&D Beyond, typically at level-up. When a change shows up on the sheet, it's ticked off by itself.
What it takes

Small, and nothing to move

About a day, built before the fight screen. The only visible change is Manage › Settings › Books.

  • A source is a book, the SRD, or someone's homebrew; it holds entries (a monster, spell, item, feat, subclass or a section of rules text), each with its page. One shape for every kind.
  • The table that will hold campaign content is still empty, so it becomes entries of a source with nothing to move.
  • A monster in a fight or an item handed out keeps a copy of its entry, so switching a book off never breaks a fight or an inventory.
  • Stays as it is: characters, play state, rolls and the story, each in its own tables.
  • Not built: a general "store anything" model, plug-ins, a different structure per book, or access checks during play.

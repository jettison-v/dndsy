1. Try the Long Rest fix on your computer

5 minutes

The Rest window now has Short Rest and Long Rest side by side at the top. Long Rest used to be a small link that did nothing when clicked, which is why it didn't work for you.

  1. Open localhost:8000, Vi's Battle (your local copy; Vi shows as connected for the next two hours).
  2. Press Rest at the right end of the Resources row (on a phone it's in the Status row).
  3. Press Long Rest at the top of the window: the list changes to what a Long Rest brings back, and the button says Take a Long Rest.
  4. Press Short Rest to switch back. Don't take the rest locally: nothing reaches D&D Beyond from your computer.

Then tell me ship it, and it goes to beta.

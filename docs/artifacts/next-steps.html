Ready now

3. Review live state on beta

After step 2

Wait for 0.9.8, so what you see is what the table will get. Then in VTT test, with one of your own characters (Vi or Spurt) and its D&D Beyond sheet open in Chrome:

  • Cast a spell from Battle: a slot goes on D&D Beyond; Undo gives it back.
  • Resources: press a resource, Use one, Give one back.
  • Rest: Short Rest and Long Rest.
  • Items: an item's side panel, Equip, Attune, Count, Give (to someone at the table). Sheet › Items, Change coins.
  • Give loot (you're an admin): Sheet › Items on a character, pick an item and some gold.
  • Prepare: Sheet › Spells › Prepare spells, or a spell's side panel for one quick swap.

Each change shows ↻ until D&D Beyond has it, then lands on your sheet within seconds. Tell me what felt wrong, or switch it on for the table.

4. Share the bridge with the table

When you're ready
  1. Send the table this link: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/askdnd-bridge/obpgemimnhngpcckodjkcihiahoaefpl (it's also in Help › FAQ). Each player adds it in Chrome on a computer.
  2. Send them the bridge's risk summary. Before you send it I'll update it for DM Backup and preparing spells, and share it from its Share menu so they can open it.
  3. On beta, open The Adventure Begins › Manage › Settings, scroll to Virtual tabletop, switch on D&D Beyond bridge, and save. Leave the others off until step 3 says they're ready.
  4. Each player: open askdnd once in that Chrome, signed in, then open their D&D Beyond sheet. The Live pill turns green: Connected.

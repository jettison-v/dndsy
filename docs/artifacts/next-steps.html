2. Say "merge 155": a bug the live test found

A word

The new live run on the real D&D Beyond found that dropping a spell D&D Beyond lists in both its 2014 and 2024 copies (Detect Evil and Good) made it vanish from Prepare spells, so it couldn't be prepared again. PR 155 fixes it. Once it's on beta I put Cathartic's two spells back and run the prepare test again. Give, loot and DM Backup passed live with 0.9.8.

4. Review live state on beta

Ready now

0.9.8 is published (Nov 2): update it in Chrome first (chrome://extensions › Developer mode › Update). Then in VTT test, with one of your own characters (Vi or Spurt) and its D&D Beyond sheet open in Chrome:

  • Cast a spell from Battle: a slot goes on D&D Beyond; Undo gives it back.
  • Resources: press a resource, Use one, Give one back.
  • Rest: Short Rest and Long Rest.
  • Items: an item's side panel, Equip, Attune, Count, Give (to someone at the table). Sheet › Items, Change coins.
  • Give loot (you're an admin): Sheet › Items on a character, pick an item and some gold.
  • Prepare: Sheet › Spells › Prepare spells, or a spell's side panel for one quick swap.

Each change shows ↻ until D&D Beyond has it, then lands on your sheet within seconds. Tell me what felt wrong, or switch it on for the table.

1. Try this round on your computer

10 minutes

One round, all together: the Long Rest fix, the Prepare spells screen you liked, the Monster Manual upload, and DM Backup (reviewed twice; the bug I found is fixed and tested). On your local copy, Cathartic and Vi show as connected for the next three hours.

  1. Long Rest: open Vi's Battle, press Rest at the right end of the Resources row, then Long Rest at the top of the window. The list changes to what a Long Rest brings back, with a link to Prepare spells.
  2. Prepare spells: open Cathartic's Prepare spells (also on Sheet › Spells). Tick and untick, watch the count and your changes, try the filters, and press Make: each change shows ↻. Locally nothing reaches D&D Beyond, so it stays like that.

  4. DM Backup needs the new extension and a real D&D Beyond to try, so it isn't something to try locally. I'll run it on the Mac Mini's test seats once Google approves 0.9.8. On beta it's only in test campaigns until you've seen it.

4. Review live state on beta

After step 3

Wait for 0.9.8, so what you see is what the table will get. Then in VTT test, with one of your own characters (Vi or Spurt) and its D&D Beyond sheet open in Chrome:

  • Cast a spell from Battle: a slot goes on D&D Beyond; Undo gives it back.
  • Resources: press a resource, Use one, Give one back.
  • Rest: Short Rest and Long Rest, as in step 1.
  • Items: an item's side panel, Equip, Attune, Count, Give (to someone at the table). Sheet › Items, Change coins.
  • Give loot (you're an admin): Sheet › Items on a character, pick an item and some gold.
  • Prepare: Sheet › Spells › Prepare spells, or a spell's side panel for one quick swap.

Each change shows ↻ until D&D Beyond has it, then lands on your sheet within seconds. Tell me what felt wrong, or switch it on for the table.

Campaigns and roles: what steps 10 and 11 change

Step 10 reorganizes the data so a character can belong to more than one campaign over time, roles become per campaign, and a DM's rulings stop editing the tool. Step 11 brings Inventory, Features and then the Campaign pages onto the list pattern. Read the pictures first; the decisions are at the bottom and save as you answer.

Step 10 · permissions

Roles move from the account to each campaign

The account keeps only what's about the whole tool: Owner and Admin set up people, characters and billing. Everything about running a table becomes a role in a campaign: DM, Reviewer or Player. An example with a second campaign that Brett runs:

What follows from that

  • In Waterdeep, Brett reads every player's notes (he's a player there) and reviews recaps.
  • In his own campaign Brett is the DM, so he can't read the notes of characters there, and if a character moves in from elsewhere, he can't read its notes either.
  • Waterdeep's DM sees nothing of the second campaign, and never sees Waterdeep's players' notes.

The notes rule, precisely

A person can't read a character's notes if they DM, or ever DMed, a campaign that character is or was in (decision 3). Story pages, recaps and the D&D Beyond Background stay visible to everyone at the table, as today.

Step 10 · build order

Four steps, each shippable and reversible

Each: a manual backup first, a migration that copies today's data into the new shape (one campaign, four characters), tests, and a check on beta.

  1. 1
    Characters separate from campaignsCharacters get their own name; a dated membership links each to its campaign and story page; Wild Shape keeps move to the membership.You see: nothing changes. The largest step (about 33 places in the code).
  2. 2
    Roles per campaignThe DM tick and Reviewer move to campaign membership; Manage › People shows tool roles and campaign roles.You see: a reshaped access chart on Manage › People.
  3. 3
    Tool corrections and house rulesToday's sheet corrections become tool corrections; a new Manage › House rules page; the roll window says "House rule" when one applies.You see: the first DM rulings, e.g. Magic Missile rolled once.
  4. 4
    More than one campaignA campaign switcher in the menu bar; story addresses carry the campaign (/c/waterdeep/sessions/20); create a campaign, add people and characters, move a character.You see: the second campaign appears.
Step 11

More lists on the Spell Book pattern

Same rows, filters, views, search, Shortlist and notes as Spells and Wild Shape. Inventory and Features don't depend on step 10. The Campaign lists do, because the campaign switcher decides which campaign's people and places they show.

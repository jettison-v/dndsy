askdnd · after a game night

From recording to recap

Your steps, start to finish. The recording and its transcript never leave your Mac: the askdnd Processor removes the table talk, and only the game goes to askdnd, which drafts the recap and keeps nothing else. You review the draft and publish it, and the table sees it on Story › Previously on….

Before the game

  1. Wear headphones.

    Without them Zoom's audio leaks back into your mic and every line is heard twice.

  2. Record the session in MacWhisper,

    which transcribes it and labels who's speaking.

After the game, on your Mac about 25 minutes, mostly waiting

  1. Export from MacWhisper.

    Export Segments as .json, with speaker names, timestamps and milliseconds on. Save it anywhere; Downloads is fine.

  2. Open askdnd Processor on your MacBook, from Applications or the Dock (not the Mac Mini: the recordings and the filter live on the MacBook).

    It opens a page in your browser (127.0.0.1:8765) that only your Mac can reach. If it says Ollama isn't running, press Start Ollama.

  3. Add the session.

    Drop the .json file on the page, set Session date to the day you played, and press Add session. Tick Replace if this date already exists only to start a date over.

  4. Step 1Filter.

    Press Filter this session. It removes Zoom's echo, then the model on your Mac marks the table talk. A full session takes about 15–20 minutes; leave the page open.

  5. Step 2Review the cuts.

    Each box is a stretch marked as table talk. Open any you're unsure of and press Keep if it has game in it: a rules call, a plan, something that happened. You're the judge; a quick skim is enough.

  6. Step 3Save.

    Press Save final transcript. It's kept on your Mac with your Keep choices applied.

  7. Step 4Send to askdnd.

    Press Send to askdnd. Only the filtered transcript goes. askdnd finds the session by its date (or makes it), drafts the recap with Claude in about a minute, and doesn't keep the transcript. The page then says when it was sent and links to the draft.

After the game, on askdnd

  1. Review the draft and publish it.

    Manage › Sessions, or the Processor's link. Read it, fix anything wrong (your fixes teach later drafts), and press Publish. Before the next game is ideal, so the table's Previously on… is ready.

  2. Go in order.

    Each draft builds on the recaps published before it, so a session drafted ahead of earlier ones knows less of the story. Publishing a session starts the next one's draft by itself.

Only step 9's filtered text leaves your Mac. The recording, MacWhisper's export and the Processor's files stay in ~/Code/dndsy/transcripts/, which is never committed to git or uploaded.

If something goes wrong

  • "No UPLOAD_TOKEN in your local .env yet": the key the Processor sends with is missing. Ask Claude; it's set with scripts/set-secret.sh UPLOAD_TOKEN, the same value on askdnd and your Mac.
  • "Couldn't reach askdnd": check the internet, then press Send to askdnd again. Nothing was lost.
  • The filter seems stuck: its progress is in the log above. If it has stopped, open the session and press Filter again.

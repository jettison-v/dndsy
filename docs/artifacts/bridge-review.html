Seeing what the bridge is doing

The bridge grew from a proof into something the whole table leans on. It's careful and it doesn't lose changes, but it tells you what it's doing in six places, in different words, and the moment a change goes wrong is the one where it says least.

Scanned Nov 3 with the real extension in Chrome and a stand-in for D&D Beyond, as Cole's player: the sheet closed, then open, a change that lands, one that D&D Beyond drops, the panel closed, the sheet closed again. 17 states, every place that shows them.

Where the bridge speaks

Live pillIn askdnd's menu bar on every page. Its colour answers "will a change reach D&D Beyond?"; it opens the Live window.
Live windowHow each sheet at the table is connected; a change that stopped is listed under its character with Try again and Undo.
Corner cardBottom right in askdnd, after anything you do: "Cole took 3", with Undo where there is one.
Panel beside the sheetDrawn by the extension on the left of your D&D Beyond sheet: Hit Points, each change and its state, what changed. It only shows: no buttons.
Chrome popupThe bridge's icon in Chrome's toolbar: Connected or not, and links to your sheet and your character.
Bridge pageaskdnd's page per character (/characters/…/bridge), opened from the panel's Open in askdnd. The first proof's page, still with its test buttons.

Already right

  • Nothing is lost or doubled. One batch at a time per character, each number read back from D&D Beyond, and nothing written over a value askdnd can't explain. In the scan, the dropped change stopped with "Nothing was written over", as it should.
  • The pill's colour is one clear answer: green (your sheet), yellow (through the DM), red (can't, while the table plays, or something to make by hand), grey.
  • A number on its way carries a small mark on the page until D&D Beyond has it.
  • A change that stopped has Try again and Undo in the Live window, for its player and the DM.
  • An extension too old for a change says to update it.

What the scan found

Says nothing

When a change doesn't go through, nothing tells you where you're looking

  • No corner card. The pill turns red and says "1 to make by hand"; that's all, unless you open the Live window.
  • Hit Points jumps back with a green "+3" (48 to 51), the same mark as healing, so the moment it failed reads like a heal.
  • The panel beside the sheet says "Try again or undo it in askdnd", sending you to another tab mid-fight.
Misleads

The ✓ comes before D&D Beyond has it

  • The corner card says "✓ Cole took 3" the moment you press Enter. Whether D&D Beyond kept it shows only as a small mark on the number, and the card is gone by then.
Says nothing

Retries are invisible

  • A dropped change is tried three times (at once, after 5 seconds, after 20). Everywhere it just says "Waiting for D&D Beyond", with no count and no time.
  • A second change waited more than a minute behind the stopped one with no reason given.
  • The Live window lists nothing until a change has given up.
Misleads

The Chrome popup always says Connected

  • It means "askdnd answers", not "your sheet is connected": it said Connected with the sheet closed and with a change to make by hand.
  • No version (you checked 0.9.8 on three profiles by hand), no word of what's on its way or stuck.
Untidy

Six vocabularies for five states

  • "Connected" and "Live with D&D Beyond"; "waiting" and "Waiting for D&D Beyond"; "to make by hand", "Make these on D&D Beyond" and "Not on D&D Beyond yet", sometimes three at once in one panel.
  • Errors are engineering text: "D&D Beyond answered OK but didn't keep it. Tried 3 times. Nothing was written over."
  • An open Live window doesn't update: it said "Not connected" while the pill above it said Connected.
Untidy

The bridge page is the first proof's

  • Open in askdnd (the panel's only button) lands on it: Take 1 hit point, Heal 1 and Drink test buttons, a third copy of the waiting list, and "What changed: Nothing yet" after two changes.

Choices for you

1. Where Try again and Undo live

You asked whether retries belong in the extension's window. They can sit in the panel beside the sheet: it's askdnd's own panel next to D&D Beyond's page, not over its controls (the rule it follows today is to show only). Chrome's popup closes as soon as you click anywhere, which makes it a poor place to act.

A · Wherever you're lookingRecommended

A red corner card in askdnd, the Live window, and the panel beside the sheet all offer Try again and Undo for a change that didn't go through. The popup says how many and opens askdnd.

B · askdnd only

The red corner card and the Live window have them; the panel says what happened and links to askdnd, as today.

C · The popup too

As A, and the Chrome popup lists each one with Try again and Undo.

2. The corner card after a change

A · The card follows the changeRecommended

"Cole took 3 · on its way" with a spinner, then ✓ "on D&D Beyond", or "trying again (2 of 3)", or red "didn't go through" with Try again and Undo. The card stays until it's settled.

B · ✓ at once, red only on failure

As today, plus a red card when a change gives up.

3. The Chrome popup

A · A status cardRecommended

Your sheet open or closed (and which tab), what's on its way, what didn't go through, the extension's version (and whether it's current), then the links. One look tells you whether the bridge is working.

B · Links, plus the version

As today, with the version and "Connected" changed to say whether your sheet is open.

4. The bridge page

A · Retire itRecommended

Open in askdnd opens your character's Battle with the Live window open. The test buttons go; the history of changes stays on Manage › askdnd › D&D Beyond bridge for admins.

B · Keep it as the history

Drop the test buttons and keep the page as each character's full list of changes, in the new words.

Without a choice: one set of words, and the quick fixes

Connected, Through the DM, Sheet closed for the sheet; On its way, Trying again (2 of 3), On D&D Beyond, Didn't go through for a change, the same in all six places. Errors in plain words ("D&D Beyond didn't keep it after 3 tries. Nothing on your sheet changed."). No gain mark when a number goes back because a change didn't go through. The open Live window updates with the pill, and a change waiting behind another says so.

What I'd build first

  1. The words and the quick fixes, in all six places (no choice needed).

  3. Try again and Undo where you're looking (choice 1), in the panel too.
  4. The popup and the bridge page (choices 3 and 4), with a new extension version.

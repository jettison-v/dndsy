Try live state

The buttons only show for a character's own player, so use a test seat and not your own login.

  1. In the Chrome profile signed in as CatharticPlayer or ViPlayer, open that character's sheet on D&D Beyond.
  2. In the same profile, open VTT test on beta and go to the character's Battle page.
  3. Cast: pick a spell in the Spell Book, press Cast and choose a level. The slot goes down on both pages. Undo on the corner card gives it back.
  4. Resources: click a resource on Battle, use one, then give it back.
  5. Rests: open Hit Points' panel and press Short Rest. It lists what comes back before anything changes.
  6. Coins: on the character page's Inventory tab, press Change coins and add 1 gold, then Undo. The Undo card lasts about 20 seconds.

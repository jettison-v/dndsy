Your picks

Five choices

Each is a real design choice, not a slip. My pick is first and green. Answer in the comments, or "all your picks".

1. One on/off control for every yes/no setting

Today: the autosaving switch (on the right on Books and Features, the left on Labs), a checkbox and Save (Accounts' Admin, Art style), a button saying its state ("Sends feedback: on"), radios and Save (Trophy Case). The switch isn't in the design system at all.

The switch, always at the row's rightIt saves itself the moment you flip it, as Settings › Features does. Added to the design system.
A checkbox and the section's SaveNothing changes until Save; slower, but undoable before saving.

2. Key moments, one look everywhere

Drawn four ways: Story's (the kind word, Decision or Reveal, above the names), the World entry's timeline (a date, no kind), a quest's steps (amber rings), the side panel's plain list. How many show differs too: 8, 3, or all 19.

Story's, with the date addedThe kind word and date in one slot, names under, the same "Show the other N" after a few, on Story, entries, quests and panels.
The World entry's timelineA dated line down the side; reads as history, but loses what kind each moment was.

3. Adding what the party learned

Three ways: a World entry has the form always open (two choices, a box, Send); a quest has "+ Add what the party learned", which jumps to its entry; Previously and Sessions have "▸ Suggest a correction or addition".

One closed "+ Add what the party learned"Opens the same form in place, on entries, quests and recaps alike; a form is never what a page rests on.
Keep the entry's form openOne press less on an entry; the others become the same fold.

4. A tab's count when there's nothing

Sessions hides its 0 while Quests and Glossary show "0"; World, the biggest list, has no count.

Hide 0, count everything elseA count means there's something there; World gets its count.
Always show the count, 0 tooEvery tab looks the same, empty ones say so.

5. A glossary term

Today it's a page of its own made to look like a window (✕, a narrower column, no title), where World entries open in the Reader.

In the Reader, inside GlossaryAs a World entry opens: same width, its own address, ↑ ↓ through the list.
A side panel over CampaignQuick to glance at without leaving where you are.
How it gets built

Three pull requests, each for your local look

  1. Bugs (the 11 layout ones), with a check in the layout tests for each so they can't come back, notably a title never breaking below a word and the Live pill's short words.
  2. The duplicated key moments: the cause in drafting, then a one-time clean-up on beta after a backup.
  3. The written rules, then your five picks, page group by page group (character pages, Campaign, Manage), each piece added to Manage › Design system where it's new (the switch, the empty state, the count rule).
Consistent already, by all three reviewers: the Battle · Sheet · Plan switch, the underline tab bars with their counts, number tiles and saves, Sheet's colour-coded section headings, Party's cards, and the condition and Concentration edges. (The sweep doesn't open windows, so Change spells and the other modals weren't in it; their own browser tests check them at every width.)

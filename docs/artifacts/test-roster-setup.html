Before you start

Everything goes into VTT test

The D&D Beyond campaign VTT test (8224496). Never the party's own campaign.

Six characters per account at most

D&D Beyond's limit. The accounts below stay at five or fewer.

A twin is named "(twin)"

Rename each copy, for example Cole (twin), so it's never mistaken for the real one on Party, in the VTT or in Table rolls.

Never your personal account

Your own Vi and Spurt stay out of testing, and nothing is claimed on that account.

The party's twins

Exact copies of the four characters your table plays, through D&D Beyond's copy and claim (or your easier way: what counts is the end state on each card).

Five for the classes nobody plays

Any sensible build at level 5 or higher; the subclass named is the point. Each is made on its account and joins VTT test directly.

When every card is green

Tell Claude "the copies are claimed". Claude then adds them to VTT test in askdnd (each played by its account's seat), checks each twin against its real character, lets each seat play several characters, and writes the live runs for each, the nightly check that tells you when a twin needs a level-up, and the coverage table in docs/testing-setup.md.

Now · step 5

What D&D Beyond's VTT is missing

The table plays on D&D Beyond's own VTT, which already handles turns. So askdnd's next job in a fight is what it leaves to the table: Wild Shape damage math worked out out loud, and forgotten effects (reordered Oct 28). Each step is one small change, tried in the test campaign before the next starts.

Your call now

Try on beta: the corner cards and badges, the compact Spell Book buttons, Your dice, Story search and dice starting together. Still to try on beta: players' rolls in D&D Beyond's Game Log (ⓐ, extension 0.9.3 loaded unpacked); the Chrome Web Store copy needs 0.9.3 next. Step 5's "ends when" and potions come after.

1

Game Mode for the table, and the Live pill

Game Mode becomes one switch for the whole table (anyone starts or ends it; it lasts 6 hours). The Live pill says how your own sheet is kept in step: in step through the bridge, read every few minutes, or something to make by hand. The bridge's health pill moves to the admins' page.

Where you'll see it: the Live pill at the top right of every page, and the window it opens. The bottom-right "D&D Beyond ·" pill goes away for players.

2

Changes only while your sheet can be reached

A control that changes a D&D Beyond number works only while that character's sheet is open with the extension; otherwise it says how, and nothing is queued. A change already waiting when the sheet goes quiet waits for it and is made by itself when it's back, ticked off if you made it by hand, and flagged if D&D Beyond changed in between.

Where you'll see it: on every control that changes a number (the HP window in step 4 first). On a phone, changes are always made on D&D Beyond.

3

A changed number marks itself

Whenever a number changes, from D&D Beyond, someone else's screen or your own, it glows for a moment with the change ("−11"). Built once, in the one way every page updates, so it works everywhere.

Where you'll see it: any number on any page as it changes: Battle, Sheet, Party.

4

Hit points with the working, aware of Wild Shape

A damage roll (the DM's for an enemy) or a healing roll (a party member's) carries its amount and type. Apply to Cole on that roll opens Cole's hit points window filled in, and askdnd works it out by the 2024 rules: the form's resistances, its Temporary Hit Points first, the Concentration save's DC. Then it makes the result on his D&D Beyond sheet through the bridge. A roll is applied once; normally the player applies it, and the DM can too. The Concentration save is the player's to roll, on their screen. Without a roll (a trap), click Hit points and enter the number.

Where you'll see it: it starts in Table rolls, the sidebar on the left of every page in Game Mode: the roll's Apply to Cole. The window opens in the middle of the page, so it works the same from any page. Without a roll: Hit points on Battle › Damage, Heal or Temporary HP. Not yet from Party cards.

Checked Oct 28: the DM's rolls reach your open sheet (the Ogre's Greatclub: to hit, damage, advantage, the total), but without the damage type, so askdnd takes it from the monster's attack in its rules data and says so ("bludgeoning (SRD 5.2.1)"), and asks when the monster isn't there.

5

Forgotten effects

Conditions that say when they end, and potions and charges at hand. "Until the end of your next turn" needs to know whose turn it is: first find out whether the extension can hear D&D Beyond's own turn tracker, as it hears rolls; if not, a simple "next turn" for the DM.

Where you'll see it: on Battle: the conditions row under the numbers, and your potions and charges beside the action columns.

Built Oct 28: Battle's edge in the newest three conditions' own colours (subdued; Blinded or Unconscious darken it), an emblem per condition in the bottom-right corner with its own colour and icon (drawn for askdnd), four signature touches, a dot per condition on your menu bar button, Concentration marked lightly in the Spell Book. Conditions sync both ways with D&D Beyond.

On beta Oct 29: one place for everything that pops up. Corner cards at the bottom right for toasts, refused reasons and others' rolls; the conditions as small badges after the name on every character page; Battle keeps its edge.

Decisions that come up later

  • Where monsters come from (2b): the SRD built in; creatures from books the DM owns entered by the DM, kept in the database.
  • How much map automation (3): decide after using simple maps.
  • Where music comes from (4): tracks the DM uploads or has rights to.
  • Video and the privacy rule (6): recording stays on your Mac, or the rule changes.
  • When askdnd.ai moves to v2: any time you say.
  • A faster bridge: count D&D Beyond's reply (with the next extension update): clear the ↻ mark on D&D Beyond's own answer to a write and check it again in the background, about 1 to 3 seconds faster. The cost: when D&D Beyond answers OK without keeping a change (it has happened), the mark clears and comes back as ! a few seconds later.

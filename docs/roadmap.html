5. Decide: change maximum hit points from askdnd (Aid)

Almost everything that changes in play already goes both ways with D&D Beyond. The one realistic gap: maximum hit points going up or down (Aid, a bonus someone sets). askdnd reads it but can't change it, so casting Aid from askdnd doesn't raise anyone's maximum; it's set on D&D Beyond. Adding it means recording the request D&D Beyond's own sheet sends, on a test seat, as was done for preparing spells; then it's small. Rarer, the same kind of work: a wizard copying a found spell into their spellbook. Say which, if either.

7. Slice 2 · askdnd runs the fight

The DM starts a fight here, adds creatures (hidden ones only the DM sees), players roll their own initiative into it, the DM presses Next turn. askdnd owns the creatures' Hit Points and conditions (they have no D&D Beyond sheet), kept as a fight and its fighters, one row each, with Undo for the DM. Reminders and Concentration count from askdnd's turns. Players see enemies as Healthy, Bloodied or Down, the default in a DM battle settings menu. This is the turn order parked on Oct 28, built with your answers from then.
